## The one-paragraph situation

Code Square is a software company in Port Said, Egypt, part of the M Tech Square group. It has a genuinely strong written identity, a sophisticated bilingual website, a published methodology, and **four real projects — two for external clients**, including a maritime crewing platform in a Suez Canal port city. Almost none of that is visible. The website serves an **expired TLS certificate** and renders nothing to search engines or AI. The Facebook page has 74 followers, never links to the website, names no client, and explains what UX means to beginners. The problem was never a lack of identity — an identity was written and then buried. The work is to **surface it, sharpen it to one audience, put a real offer behind it, and fix the plumbing underneath.**
