# Social Media Playbook — Code Square

## For future Claude

Per-platform operating manual. What to post, when, how to write it, and — the part currently missing entirely — what to do when someone actually responds.

The most urgent section is **§6, the Facebook page fix**. The page today has a generic bio, a cover image listing seven services, **no website link at all**, and no CTA button (`stated` — `[[2026-09-02 - Facebook Page Evidence]]`). Fixing the page is a two-hour job that no amount of posting substitutes for. Do that before touching the calendar.

Priority order across platforms is argued in `[[Marketing Strategy]]` §5. Do not re-derive it here.

---

## 1. Facebook

**Priority: 1.** The buyer is here. Paid geo-targeting is cheapest here. It is also the page that already exists with 74 followers (`stated`).

**Audience:** owners and operations managers of shipping agencies, crewing/manning agents, freight forwarders, and customs brokers in Port Said, Damietta, Suez, and Ismailia. Typically 35–60, Arabic-first, mobile-first, running the business personally. Secondary: education and training-centre owners (beachhead B), and the founder's professional network.

**Format specs:**
| Format | Spec |
|---|---|
| Feed image | 1080×1350 (4:5 — occupies more vertical screen than square) |
| Carousel | 1080×1080, 6–10 frames |
| Reel | 1080×1920, 9:16, under 60s, burned-in Arabic subtitles |
| Cover | 1640×856 desktop; the safe area is the centre ~1090×630 — text outside it is cropped on mobile |
| Profile | 320×320 |
| Link preview | 1200×630 |

**Cadence (`proposed`):** 3 posts per week is the floor and the ceiling for months 1–3. More than three at this audience size does not increase reach; it dilutes attention and burns the production team. Composition per week: 1 case study or proof post, 1 beachhead pain/solution post, 1 daylight or POV post.

**Best times (Egypt/Gulf, `proposed` — treat as a starting hypothesis and replace with the page's own Insights data after four weeks):**
- Sunday–Thursday, **1:00–3:00 pm** (the post-lunch phone check) and **8:00–10:00 pm** (the strongest window).
- Friday morning is dead for B2B. Friday evening is acceptable for soft content.
- Saturday is a real working day in the Gulf and a weekend day in Egypt — treat as medium.
- Ramadan inverts everything: post after Iftar, roughly 9:00 pm–1:00 am.

**Caption architecture:**
```
LINE 1  — the hook. Fewer than 12 words. Visible before "see more". This line does 80% of the work.
LINE 2  — blank
BLOCK 1 — the specific situation, 2–4 short lines. Name the industry.
BLOCK 2 — what happened / what we did / the number.
BLOCK 3 — the takeaway in one sentence.
LAST    — the CTA: the Diagnostic Session, or a direct question.
```
Short lines. White space. A Facebook caption written as one paragraph is not read on a phone.

**Hashtag policy — be honest:** hashtags on Facebook do close to nothing. They do not meaningfully surface a post to non-followers the way they do on Instagram or LinkedIn. **Use 0–3, or none at all.** A caption ending in fifteen hashtags reads as amateur to a B2B buyer and buys no distribution. If any are used, make them brand or campaign markers — `#CodeSquare`, `#بورسعيد` — not keyword stuffing. Distribution on Facebook comes from three things instead: shares, comments in the first hour, and paid reach.

## 2. LinkedIn

**Priority: 2.** Not a lead source in the first 90 days. Its jobs are (a) verification — someone searches the company after a meeting and finds a real presence, and (b) outbound to larger agencies and the Gulf market.

**Audience:** operations and crewing managers, agency owners at the larger end, Gulf enterprise buyers, and potential hires.

**The founder profile matters more than the company page.** At this size people follow people, and LinkedIn's distribution favours personal profiles heavily. `proposed`: the founder posts, the company page reshares.

**Format specs:** image 1200×627 · document/carousel PDF 1080×1080 (the highest-reach format on the platform) · video with subtitles under 90s · text post 1,300–2,000 characters.

**Cadence:** 1–2 posts per week. Language: **English primarily**, Arabic for Egypt-specific content. This is where the Jeddah/Riyadh service areas (`stated`) finally get content behind them.

**Caption architecture:** hook line → line break → 3–5 short paragraphs → one question. LinkedIn truncates after ~3 lines; the first two lines carry the post.

**Hashtags:** 3–5, genuinely used here, and industry-specific: `#maritime` `#shipping` `#crewing` `#logistics` `#softwaredevelopment`.

**What works here that does not work on Facebook:** the founder's first-person story of building a system for a manning agency; a document carousel of the four-phase methodology; an honest post about a scope decision. What does not work: the same Arabic caption pasted over.

## 3. WhatsApp Business

**Priority: 1.** This is where deals actually close in Egyptian B2B, and it is currently a bare phone number with no configuration (`stated`).

**Setup checklist (do this in week 1):**
- [ ] Convert to a WhatsApp **Business** account
- [ ] Business profile: name, category, description, address (شارع ممفيس، Port Said), email, website link, hours
- [ ] **Greeting message** — auto-sent on first contact
- [ ] **Away message** — outside working hours, with a real return time
- [ ] **Quick replies** for the 8 most common questions (pricing, timelines, what a Diagnostic Session is, do you work outside Port Said, do you have examples)
- [ ] **Labels** as a lightweight CRM: `جديد` · `مؤهل` · `جلسة متحجوزة` · `عرض سعر` · `عميل` · `مش مناسب`
- [ ] **Catalogue** listing the three offer-ladder rungs (`[[Offer Ladder]]`)
- [ ] A `wa.me` link in the Facebook bio, the FB CTA button, and the website

**Greeting message (`proposed`, Arabic):**
> أهلاً بحضرتك في Code Square 👋
> إحنا بنبني أنظمة تشغيل مخصصة للشركات — الحجز، التوظيف، المتابعة، والتقارير.
> عشان نقدر نساعدك بسرعة، ابعتلنا:
> ١. اسم الشركة ونوع نشاطها
> ٢. الشغل اللي بتحبوا تظبطوه دلوقتي بيتعمل إزاي (إكسيل؟ ورق؟ واتساب؟)
> ٣. إيه أكتر حاجة بتضيع وقت
> وهنرد عليك خلال ٢٤ ساعة بأول تشخيص مبدئي.

**Away message (`proposed`):**
> شكراً لرسالتك. إحنا حالياً خارج مواعيد العمل (الأحد–الخميس، ٩ص–٦م).
> هنرد على حضرتك أول يوم عمل. ولو الموضوع مستعجل، اكتب "عاجل" في رسالة تانية.

**Never:** broadcast lists to people who did not opt in. It is the fastest way to get a business number reported and restricted, and it destroys the one channel that actually converts.

## 4. Instagram

**Priority: 4.** Repurposing destination only. Do not build a separate strategy (`[[Marketing Strategy]]` §5.2).

**Audience:** not the maritime buyer. Younger, design-aware, useful for recruiting and for the group's visual craft. Also where beachhead B (education) partly lives.

**Specs:** feed 1080×1350 · Reels 1080×1920 · Stories 1080×1920 · carousel up to 10.
**Cadence:** 2–3 per week, all repurposed. Zero original production.
**Hashtags:** unlike Facebook, these do work. 8–15, mixed: niche (`#شركات_برمجيات` `#بورسعيد`), format (`#uiux`), and broad. Put them in the first comment or below a line break.
**Bio:** must carry the same one-liner and the same `wa.me` link as everywhere else.

## 5. Comment and DM response playbook

**The SLA (`proposed`, and it must be honoured because the website already promises it):**
| Surface | Response time |
|---|---|
| Comment on a post | Under 1 hour during working hours; always same day |
| DM / Messenger | Under 2 hours during working hours; under 12 hours otherwise |
| WhatsApp | Under 2 hours during working hours |
| Website form | Under 4 working hours, against a 24-hour public promise |

The Responder is a **named person with named hours** (`[[Foundation Brief]]` §10 lists this as undecided — decide it).

### The response ladder

**1. Every comment gets a reply.** Even "👍". Especially "👍" — early engagement is what makes the platform show a post to more people, and at 74 followers every single comment is a meaningful percentage of the audience.

**2. Reply with substance, not thanks.** "شكراً" ends a conversation. A question continues it.
- Weak: `شكراً لتعليقك 🙏`
- Strong: `تسلم. سؤال — الشغل ده عندكم دلوقتي بيتعمل على إكسيل ولا على نظام؟`

**3. A buying-intent comment moves to DM within one reply.**
> `سؤال كويس، وإجابته بتختلف حسب حجم شغلكم. بعتلك رسالة على الخاص عشان نتكلم بالتفصيل.`
Then actually send it, within minutes.

**4. The DM → WhatsApp handoff.** Messenger is where the conversation starts; WhatsApp is where business gets done. Move it deliberately.
> `عشان نسهّل التواصل، ممكن نكمل على واتساب؟ الرقم: +20 10 43547526 — أو ابعتلي رقمك وأنا اللي أكلمك.`
Offering to call them is the stronger option: it removes the effort from their side and it filters out non-serious enquiries.

**5. The qualification questions** (in WhatsApp, conversationally, never as a form):
1. الشركة بتشتغل في إيه بالظبط؟
2. الشغل اللي حابين تظبطوه بيتعمل إزاي دلوقتي؟
3. إيه أكتر حاجة بتضيع وقت أو بتسبب أخطاء؟
4. مين اللي هياخد القرار في الموضوع ده؟
5. عايزين النظام يبدأ يشتغل امتى؟

**6. The Diagnostic Session invitation** — the destination of every good conversation:
> `اللي بتقوله ده بنشوفه كتير في الشغل ده. أقترح نعمل جلسة تشخيص، ٣٠–٤٥ دقيقة، مجاناً. هنمشي على شغلكم خطوة بخطوة، وفي الآخر هستلم حضرتك ورقة مكتوبة فيها المشاكل الأساسية والترتيب اللي أنصح بيه — تفضل معاك سواء اشتغلنا مع بعض أو لأ. إيه أنسب يوم لحضرتك؟`

**7. Pricing questions in public comments.** Never post a price, never dodge:
> `بيختلف حسب حجم الشغل — نظام بسيط غير نظام فيه تتبع وتقارير. لو تحب، ابعتلي على الخاص تفاصيل شغلكم وأديك تقدير حقيقي مش رقم عشوائي.`
Real pricing is `TBD` (`[[Foundation Brief]]` §6) — the `$19/$49/$99` on the SaaS infographic is **mockup art and must never be quoted to anyone.**

**8. Off-fit enquiries** (a 500 EGP logo, a student's project). Decline warmly and quickly; refer where possible. A courteous no in ninety seconds protects the hours that matter and occasionally comes back as a referral.

## 6. Facebook page optimisation checklist

**Do this in week 1. It is the highest return-per-hour work available.**

### 6.1 The bio

Current (`stated`):
> Code Square (CS) شريكك التكنولوجي لبناء حلول رقمية ذكية (تطبيقات – منصات – AI). بنبدأ بفهم البزنس ونحوّل أفكارك لمنتجات قابلة للتوسع. ببساطة: بنبني بيزنسك رقميًا ونكبره معاك

**What is wrong with it:** "شريكك التكنولوجي" is what every agency in Egypt says (`[[Foundation Brief]]` §2, Layer 3). It names a capability list, not a buyer. It contains no proof, no place, and no next step. Someone in the shipping business reads it and sees nothing addressed to them.

**Replacement (`proposed`, beachhead = maritime/logistics):**
> بنبني النظام اللي شغلك بيمشي عليه — وبنفضل شغالين عليه معاك.
> فريق المنتج والهندسة الخارجي لشركتك. متخصصين في شركات الملاحة، التوكيلات، والتوظيف البحري واللوجستيات.
> شغلنا: نظام إدارة توظيف وتتبع لوكالة ملاحية في بورسعيد · نظام حجز وإدارة لشركة سفر · منصة تعليمية كاملة بتطبيق موبايل.
> 📍 بورسعيد · 🌐 [website link] · 💬 واتساب: +20 10 43547526
> ابدأ بجلسة تشخيص مجانية ٣٠ دقيقة.

**Structure of that replacement, so it can be rewritten if the beachhead changes:** what we build → who for → the proof → where we are → the one next step.

## 7. Community management rules

1. **Reply to everything.** At this size there is no comment volume problem to solve.
2. **Reply as a person, not a brand.** Sign with a first name where it fits.
3. **Never argue.** If someone is wrong in public, offer the correction once, calmly, with a reason, then stop.
4. **Never post a price publicly.**
5. **Never disclose client details** — not the fee, not the timeline, not their internal problems, beyond what is in an approved case study.
6. **Never comment on politics, religion, or football.** In Egypt these are engagement traps with no upside for a B2B vendor.
7. **Do not delete criticism.** Deleting turns a comment into a story.
8. **Do delete** spam, insults, and anything targeting an individual.
9. **Engage outward.** Comment thoughtfully on posts from port-services companies, local business groups, and the client companies. At 74 followers, outbound commenting reaches more of the right people than posting does.
10. **Do not buy followers or engagement.** It poisons every ad audience the page will ever build.

## 8. Crisis and negative-comment protocol

**Tier 1 — a fair complaint or criticism.**
Reply publicly within one hour, acknowledge without defensiveness, move to private, then return publicly with the resolution if there is one.
> `كلامك في محله ومتشكر إنك قلته. ابعتلي رسالة على الخاص أو على واتساب على +20 10 43547526 وأنا هتابع الموضوع بنفسي.`

**Tier 2 — an unfair or hostile comment.**
Reply once, factually, without heat. Then stop. Do not reply twice; a second reply gives the thread another day of life.
> `مش ده اللي حصل من ناحيتنا، بس مستعدين نراجع أي تفصيلة. ابعتلنا على الخاص.`

**Tier 3 — spam, insults, competitor attacks.** Hide or delete, block, no reply.

**Tier 4 — a real incident** (a client's data exposed, a system down, a wrong claim published, a permission breach).
1. **Stop publishing immediately.** Pause every scheduled post — nothing looks worse than a cheerful post above a live problem.
2. Founder informs the affected client **directly and first**, before any public statement.
3. Assess and write down what actually happened before saying anything publicly.
4. If public comment is needed: state what happened, what is being done, and by when. No excuses, no passive voice, no legal fog.
5. Log it in the vault and change the process that allowed it.

**Two absolute rules:** the Founder handles Tier 4, not the Responder. And nobody, at any tier, replies while angry — write it, wait ten minutes, re-read, then send.

## 9. Cross-platform consistency

The single most damaging finding in `[[Foundation Brief]]` §2 is that the website and Facebook look and sound like two different companies. These must be identical everywhere, checked monthly:

- [ ] The one-liner: **بنبني النظام اللي شغلك بيمشي عليه — وبنفضل شغالين عليه معاك.**
- [ ] The same profile image
- [ ] The same colour system — website tokens, everywhere (`[[Foundation Brief]]` §8)
- [ ] The same fonts — Tajawal for Arabic, Playfair Display for display
- [ ] The same phone, WhatsApp, and email
- [ ] The same website link, present on every profile
- [ ] The same single CTA: the Diagnostic Session
- [ ] **One methodology only** — the website's four phases: التأسيس → المعمارية → الهندسة → الإطلاق والتطوير. The five-stage Facebook model is retired and must never be published again
