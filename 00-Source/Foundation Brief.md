# Foundation Brief — Code Square

## For future Claude

**This is the canonical spine of the entire vault.** Every other note — profile, messaging, ICP, content calendar, sales script, pricing — must be consistent with this file. If a note contradicts this brief, the brief wins until the founder explicitly changes it.

Facts here come from two immutable evidence notes: `[[2026-09-02 - Facebook Page Evidence]]` and `[[2026-09-02 - Website Evidence (mtechsquare.com-code-square)]]`. Strategy here is a *proposal* dated 2026-09-02 and is labelled as such.

**Revision 2 note:** revision 1 was written before the website was known and concluded Code Square had "no identity and no proof". That was wrong. A strong written identity and four real projects already exist — they are buried behind a broken website and never surfaced on social. The corrected diagnosis is below. Do not reuse revision-1 framing.

## 0. Rules every note in this vault must obey

1. **Never fabricate.** No invented client names, revenue figures, headcounts, years-in-business, or "trusted by 200+ companies". Unknown → write `TBD` and add it to `[[Open Questions and Decisions Needed]]`.
2. **Label confidence.** `stated` (from the page, the site, or the founder) · `proposed` (strategy invented 2026-09-02) · `TBD`.
3. **Bilingual.** Arabic (Egyptian dialect for social, Modern Standard for formal) and English. The website already ships both — keep parity. Never machine-translate; write each language natively.
4. **Capability lists are not messages.** "Mobile, web, SaaS, AI, UI/UX, integration, support" belongs in the service catalog, never in a headline.
5. **Every claim needs a proof path.** Name the artifact that backs it — a project, a screenshot, a metric, a client quote. No proof → no claim.
6. **Two published frameworks already exist and conflict** (site's 4-phase methodology vs Facebook's 5-stage model). Pick one canonical version; never publish both again.

## 2. The corrected diagnosis (proposed, 2026-09-02)

Code Square does not have an identity problem. It has a **visibility, coherence, and conversion problem**, in that order.

**Layer 1 — The proof is invisible (technical).**
The website is unreachable in practice: an **expired TLS certificate** puts a browser security warning in front of every visitor, and **Angular client-side rendering with no prerender** means Google and every AI engine see a blank page. No sitemap, no robots.txt, no page-level meta. The single best asset the company owns cannot be found or trusted. *Nothing else on this list matters until this is fixed.*

**Layer 2 — Two companies wearing one name (coherence).**
The website sells systems engineering and an Outsourced CTO relationship to growing enterprises, with four named projects behind it. Facebook explains what UX means to beginners, names zero clients, and never links to the site. Even the colour systems differ (site: navy `#001F3F` + gold `#D5B182` + purple `#A855F7`; Facebook: violet on near-black, no gold). A prospect meeting both channels meets two different vendors.

**Layer 3 — No position (strategy).**
"شريكك التكنولوجي" is what every agency in Egypt says. Seven services × unlimited audiences × 74 followers = zero recall. The company has genuine, unusual assets — maritime/logistics software, a live education platform, a pharmacist founder — and points none of them at a specific buyer.

**Layer 4 — No offer, no conversion path.**
A follower's only action is to message and hope. There is no named, priced, bookable first step. The website form promises a 24-hour SLA with no owner and no CRM behind it.

**Layer 5 — Proof without numbers.**
Four real projects are described by *what they are*, never by *what changed for the client*. No before/after, no metric, no quote, no logo permission on file.

## 3. Positioning (proposed — founder must approve)

> **Code Square is the outsourced product and engineering team for businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and off-the-shelf tools — we design and build the operating system your business actually runs on, then keep running it with you.**

Lead with **"Outsourced CTO / تكنولوجيا كاملة بدون ما توظف فريق"** — the language is already on the site and it is the one claim a freelancer and a template shop cannot make.

Four assets that are genuinely not copyable by a generic Port Said agency:

| Asset | Why it is an unfair advantage |
|---|---|
| **Osama Sakr Manning Agency** | Working software in **maritime crewing** — recruitment, tracking, live dashboards — built in **Port Said**, on the Suez Canal. There is a dense, wealthy, underserved cluster of shipping agencies, crewing agents, freight forwarders, and customs brokers within driving distance, and CS already has a reference in it. |
| **Techno Square + Online Techno Square** | A live education platform, mobile app, dashboard, and enrolment flow already in production. A proven pattern sellable to every academy and training centre — with no client approval needed to demo it. |
| **The M Tech Square group** | A permanent in-house case-study factory. CS can ship and publish real product work without waiting for a client's legal sign-off. |
| **A pharmacist founder** | Real domain fluency in pharmacy, clinics, and medical workflows. Converts "we build software" into "we understand your dispensing workflow". Cannot be faked by a competitor. |

## 4. Beachhead markets (proposed — pick ONE to lead)

Do not launch all of these. Rank, then start with one.

| # | Segment | Why | Proof held today |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Maritime, logistics & port services** (shipping agencies, crewing/manning agents, freight forwarders, customs brokers) | Highest-value B2B in the company's own city. Real budgets, painful manual workflows, almost no local software vendor speaks their language. **CS already shipped one.** | **Osama Sakr Manning Agency** — direct, nameable reference. |
| **B** | **Education** (academies, training centres, private teachers) | The proof is already built and operating. Fastest path to a demo-able sale. Lower ticket size than A. | **Techno Square + Online Techno Square** — live platform, mobile app, dashboard. |
| **C** | **Healthcare** (pharmacies, clinics, medical centres) | Founder's own domain and network. Highest long-term margin. Zero local competition with this fluency. | None yet — needs one pilot. |
| **D** | Professional offices (lawyers, accountants) | Named in the old plan. Real need, but no domain edge and no proof. | None. |

**Recommendation:** lead with **A (maritime/logistics)** — it is the highest-ticket segment, it is on the company's doorstep, and it is the only one where a competitor cannot follow. Run **B (education)** as the volume/demo engine in parallel since the asset already exists. Open **C (healthcare)** with one founder-network pilot to create the reference. **D is a later expansion, not a launch market.**

## 6. The offer ladder (proposed)

A follower currently has nothing to buy. Three rungs:

1. **Free — جلسة تشخيص (Diagnostic Session, 30–45 min).** Named, bookable, with a written one-page output the prospect keeps whether or not they buy. This is the entire top of funnel and the only CTA that should appear on social.
2. **Paid, small — خريطة المشروع (Blueprint, fixed price, 1–2 weeks).** Problem definition, user journey, feature priority, phased scope, fixed build quote. De-risks the big decision, gets money on day one, fully creditable against the build.
3. **Build & run — MVP → versions → retainer.** Fixed-scope V1, then a monthly retainer for support and iteration. This is where the "Outsourced CTO" positioning is actually delivered and where the margin lives.

Pricing is **TBD** — the founder must set it. The `$19/$49/$99` on the SaaS infographic is mockup art and must never be quoted. The website's budget dropdown bands are unknown and must be confirmed.

## 7. Proof strategy (proposed)

Ranked by how fast it can exist:

1. **Publish the four projects that already exist**, each rewritten as problem → approach → result **with at least one number**. Get written permission from El Shoush and Osama Sakr; the two internal projects need no permission.
2. **Go get the numbers.** Ask both external clients: hours saved per week, bookings/placements processed, errors eliminated, time-to-quote before vs after. One number converts more than a page of adjectives.
3. **One healthcare pilot** from the founder's own network, traded for a documented before/after and a filmed testimonial.
4. **Build in daylight.** Publish discovery documents, scoping questions, wireframes, architecture decisions. Process transparency is proof while results are still thin — and it is the only content type that credibly signals "systems engineers".

- **Authoritative tokens (from the compiled site CSS):** ground `#001F3F` navy · group accent gold `#D5B182` / `#E8C9A0` · **Code Square accent `#A855F7`** · glass `rgba(255,255,255,.03)` with gold border · fonts Playfair Display (display) + Tajawal (Arabic).
- **Conflict to resolve:** Facebook creatives use violet-on-near-black with no gold. Two visual systems, one name. **Decision required:** adopt the website tokens as canonical and rebuild social templates on them, or formally split the group look from the CS look. Recommend the former.
- **Creative rule going forward:** the current infographics carry 8 numbered bullets on one dark image — unreadable in a phone feed. **One idea per creative, maximum 15 words on the image.** Detail moves to the caption.
