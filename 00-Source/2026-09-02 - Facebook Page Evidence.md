## Published case study — M Tech Square × Code Square

A ~10-part carousel case study (posted from ~2026-08-24 onward, latest 2026-08-31). Verbatim narrative arc:

**Part 1 — the problem.** First meeting question was not "what should the website look like?" but **"إيه اللي ناقص؟"** Finding: a company that builds digital presence for others had no digital presence of its own. Deeper finding: M Tech Square is a **parent company with multiple entities underneath**, each with its own service and audience, but clients only ever saw one fragment. Sales/marketing relied on verbal explanation, presentations, and printed materials — no digital portfolio.

**Part 2 — structure & audience.** Audience is not Egypt-only; digital services are geography-independent. Site must be **Arabic & English**, not just translated — a full parallel experience. Core design idea: **Universe** — M Tech Square is the universe, each entity is a planet with its own world, audience, and personality, but all orbiting the same space. "مختلفين… لكن مش منفصلين."

**Part 3 — per-entity worlds.**
- **M Tech Square (the Universe)** — space as visual concept; planets = each entity has distinct value and role.
- **Code Square** — the site itself is the proof. 3D models + animations so the technology is part of the experience. Message: solutions are **custom-built for the client's needs, not a template**. "Custom-built. مش Copy & Paste."
- **Techno Square** — education as a journey. Images, illustrations, infographics for a student audience. Mascot **تاتا 🤖** used as a journey companion, not decoration.
- **Online Techno Square** — distance still deserves an experience. Glassmorphism + holographic elements. Online form wired directly into the Techno Square dashboard.
