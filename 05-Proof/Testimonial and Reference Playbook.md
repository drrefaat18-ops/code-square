# Testimonial and Reference Playbook

## For future Claude

Code Square currently has **zero client quotes** and **two Facebook reviews of unknown content**. This note is the operating manual for changing that: when to ask, the exact words to use, and the question design that produces a specific, quotable answer instead of "شغل ممتاز شكرًا".

Everything here is `proposed` (2026-09-02) except the two facts about the Facebook reviews, which come from `[[2026-09-02 - Facebook Page Evidence]]`.

**Absolute rule:** never draft a testimonial and ask a client to approve it. Even when a client says "اكتبها إنت وأنا أوافق" — which they often will — the answer is: *"أنا هبعتلك سؤال واحد، اكتب إجابتك بكلامك إنت، وأنا هظبط الصياغة وأرجعهالك."* Their words are the entire value. A quote you wrote is marketing copy wearing a client's name, and a reader can hear the difference.

Permission mechanics — forms, consent boxes, storage — live in `[[Client Permission and Evidence Pack]]`. This note covers the asking.

---

## 1. When to ask

| Moment | Why it works | What to ask for |
|---|---|---|
| **The "it works" moment** — the client says something positive unprompted, in WhatsApp, mid-project | Highest-emotion moment in the whole relationship, and it is already written down | Nothing. **Screenshot it.** Then ask permission to use it later. This is the cheapest testimonial there is. |
| **2–4 weeks after launch** | Long enough to have used it, short enough to remember the "before" | Full testimonial + the metric |
| **After a support win** — you fixed something fast | Gratitude peak | Testimonial, or a Facebook review |
| **At a renewal or a new phase** | They just voted with money | Reference-call consent + referral ask |
| **Never:** during a delay, an unpaid invoice, or an open complaint | Obvious, but it happens | — |

**Rule of one:** ask for **one** thing per message. A message asking for a testimonial, a metric, a logo, and a referral gets none of them.

## 2. The ask scripts

### 2a. WhatsApp — Arabic (the default; use this one)

> أستاذ [الاسم] 🙏
> النظام بقاله [مدة] شغال معاكم دلوقتي، وحابب أسألك سؤال واحد بس:
>
> **لو صاحبك عنده نفس المشكلة اللي كانت عندك وسألك عننا — هتقوله إيه؟**
>
> اكتبهالي بكلامك إنت عادي، ولو حابب تبعتها Voice Note أسهل. وأنا هظبط الصياغة وأرجعهالك تشوفها قبل أي حاجة تتنشر.

### 2b. WhatsApp — English

> Hi [Name],
> The system has been running for [duration] now, and I have just one question:
>
> **If a friend had the same problem you had and asked you about us — what would you tell them?**
>
> Write it however you like, or send a voice note if that is easier. I will tidy the wording and send it back for your approval before anything is published.

### 2c. Email — Arabic (formal / MSA, for a Gulf or corporate client)

> الموضوع: سؤال واحد عن تجربتكم مع Code Square
>
> تحية طيبة أستاذ [الاسم]،
>
> مضى على تشغيل النظام [المدة]، ونعمل حاليًا على توثيق تجارب عملائنا.
>
> سؤال واحد فقط: **لو سألكم زميل في نفس المجال عن تجربتكم معنا، بماذا ستجيبون؟**
>
> إجابتكم بصيغتكم الخاصة هي المطلوب تمامًا. لن يُنشر أي نص قبل اعتمادكم له كتابيًا بالحرف.
>
> شاكرين لكم،
> [الاسم] — Code Square

### 2d. Email — English

> Subject: One question about working with Code Square
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> The system has now been live for [duration], and we are documenting our clients' experiences.
>
> One question: **if a colleague in your industry asked you about working with us, what would you say?**
>
> Your own wording is exactly what we want. Nothing is published until you approve the exact text in writing.
>
> Thank you,
> [Name] — Code Square

## 3. Question design — how to get a usable quote

The reason most testimonials are useless is that the question was useless. "How was your experience?" produces "great, thanks". These questions produce sentences you can publish.

| Ask this | Because it produces |
|---|---|
| **لو صاحبك عنده نفس المشكلة وسألك عننا، هتقوله إيه؟** | The single best question. Reframes it as advice to a peer, not praise for a vendor — people are specific when advising a friend. |
| **إيه أول حاجة لاحظتها اتغيرت بعد ما النظام اشتغل؟** | A concrete before/after, often with a number attached for free |
| **كنت خايف من إيه قبل ما تبدأ معانا؟ وحصل ولا لأ؟** | Handles the prospect's objection **in the client's voice**. This is the highest-converting testimonial type there is. |
| **في حاجة بقيت تعملها ما كنتش تقدر تعملها خالص قبل كده؟** | A capability statement, not an adjective |
| **إيه اللي كان مختلف في شغلنا عن أي حد تاني اشتغلت معاه؟** | Differentiation in their words — feeds `[[Brand Positioning]]` |

**Never ask:** "هل أنت راضٍ عن الخدمة؟" (yes/no) · "إيه رأيك في الشركة؟" (invites adjectives) · "ممكن تقول إننا محترفين؟" (puts words in their mouth — and the resulting quote is worthless).

**Editing rules for the answer:**
- Trim for length, never for meaning. Cut filler, keep their phrasing and their dialect.
- Never add a claim that was not in their answer — especially a number.
- Send the edited version back and get an explicit written "موافق" before publishing. Store that message (`[[Client Permission and Evidence Pack]]` §5).
- Publish in the language they spoke. An Arabic quote stays Arabic; add an English translation underneath if needed, labelled as a translation.
- Attribute fully: **name, role, company**. An unattributed quote ("عميل راضٍ") persuades nobody and reads as invented. If the client will not be named, use role and company type — "مدير عمليات، وكالة تطقيم بحري ببورسعيد" — and say why the name is withheld.

## 4. Video testimonials

Worth far more than text, and the barrier is lower than the founder thinks — the client already has the only equipment needed.

**When to ask:** only after a written testimonial has already been given. Someone who has written the sentence will say it on camera; a cold video ask usually gets a no.

**The ask:**
> أستاذ [الاسم]، الكلام اللي حضرتك كتبتهولي ده جامد جدًا 🙏 ممكن أطلب طلب أخير: تسجله فيديو قصير بالموبايل؟ دقيقة واحدة بالظبط، من مكتبك عادي، ومن غير أي تحضير. ولو مش مريح خالص، الكلام المكتوب كفاية جدًا ومفيش أي مشكلة.

**Give them the structure — an unstructured client rambles for four minutes and it is unusable:**
1. اسمك، ومنصبك، والشركة (10 ثواني)
2. المشكلة اللي كانت عندك قبل النظام (20 ثانية)
3. إيه اللي اتغير بعده (20 ثانية)
4. هتنصح مين بيه (10 ثواني)

**Practical guidance to send with it:** film **vertical** (it is going to Facebook and Instagram); sit near a window with the light on the face, not behind it; **audio matters more than picture** — a quiet room beats a good camera; one take is fine, mistakes are fine, do not re-record for perfection; if the office is noisy, a car is an excellent recording booth.

**Never** post a video before the client has watched the final cut and approved it. Subtitle every video in Arabic — most social viewing is silent.

## 5. The referenceable-client list

A written testimonial persuades. A **live phone call between two people in the same industry** closes. For the maritime beachhead this is the single strongest sales asset the company could own.

Maintain a simple list — `❓ NEEDS FROM FOUNDER:` decide where it lives (CRM, spreadsheet, or a vault note):

| Client | Segment | Consented to reference calls | Max calls per quarter | Best contact + channel | Last used |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Osama Sakr Manning Agency | Maritime crewing | ❌ not asked | — | `❓` | never |
| El Shoush | Travel | ❌ not asked | — | `❓` | never |
| Techno Square | Education | internal — always available | n/a | `❓` | never |
| M Tech Square | Corporate | internal — always available | n/a | `❓` | never |

**Rules:**
- Reference consent is a **tick box on the consent form**, asked for at the same time as the testimonial.
- **Cap it: two calls per client per quarter, maximum.** Burning a reference client is a permanent loss for a short-term gain.
- Always warn the reference *before* the prospect calls: who is calling, from where, and what they will likely ask.
- Always thank them afterwards and tell them what happened. A reference who never hears the outcome stops answering.
- Match the segment. A travel agency reference does not help a shipping agency; a manning agency reference is worth a signed contract.

## 6. The referral motion

The company's four clients are its only distribution channel that costs nothing. Nobody has ever been asked.

**When:** immediately after a positive testimonial, or after a support win. Never in the same message as the testimonial ask.

**The ask — specific, not open (this is the whole trick):**

> ❌ "لو تعرف حد محتاج خدماتنا قوله علينا" → produces nothing, every time.
>
> ✅ **"في وكالة ملاحية أو مكتب تخليص جمركي في بورسعيد تعرفه شخصيًا وشايف إن الشغل بيتعطل عندهم زي ما كان بيتعطل عندك؟ لو تعرفني بحد واحد بس، ده هيفرق معايا جدًا."**

Naming the segment, the city, and **one** person is what makes it answerable. "Anyone who needs software" is not a question a human brain can process.

**Make the introduction easy — send a forwardable message they can paste without writing anything:**

> **رسالة جاهزة للتحويل:**
> "الشركة دي اسمها Code Square، بورسعيد. هما اللي عملولنا النظام اللي بنشتغل عليه. لو حابب تسمع منهم، رقمهم [الرقم]."

`❓ NEEDS FROM FOUNDER:` Is there any referral incentive — a discount on the referrer's retainer, a fee, or nothing? Decide before asking, and never offer one retroactively. *(Recommendation: for a company this size, nothing. In a city this size a referral is a relationship favour, and attaching money to it can cheapen the ask.)*

## 7. The two existing Facebook reviews

**Stated:** the page shows **2 reviews**. Their content, rating, and authors are unrecorded.

`❓ NEEDS FROM FOUNDER:` **Open the page and read them.** Then record here: who wrote them, what rating, what they say, and whether the reviewers are real clients or friends of the company.

**Then act on what they are:**

| If they are… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Positive and from a **real client** | Reply publicly and specifically ("شكرًا يا [اسم] — سعداء إن نظام الحجز بقى أسرع عندكم 🙏"). Then ask that person for the full testimonial in §2 — someone who already reviewed you publicly will almost always say yes. |
| Positive but from a **friend, not a client** | Leave them. Do not delete, do not amplify, never quote them as client proof. |
| Negative or neutral | Reply publicly within 24 hours: acknowledge, do not argue, move the detail to a private channel, and post the resolution publicly once it is resolved. A well-handled negative review persuades more than a five-star one. |

**Going forward:** reply to **every** review within 24 hours. Two reviews is a small enough number that each one is visible to every visitor.

**A review is not a testimonial.** A Facebook review is public and uncontrolled; a testimonial is approved, attributed, and reusable in proposals. Ask for both — the review builds the page, the testimonial builds the sale.
