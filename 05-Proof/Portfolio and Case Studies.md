## 2. What this portfolio proves (proposed, 2026-09-02)

**It proves these four things, honestly and today:**

1. **Code Square ships whole systems, not pages.** Two of the four are multi-module platforms with dashboards, mobile apps, and integrations. This is the evidence behind "مهندسو أنظمة / systems engineers" and behind the Outsourced CTO positioning in `[[Foundation Brief]]` §3. A template shop cannot show this.
2. **Real external clients exist.** Two of the four are external B2B engagements. This alone answers the most common objection a Port Said buyer will raise: "have you done this before for someone who isn't you?"
3. **Bilingual and multi-language delivery is routine.** M Tech Square (a full AR + EN parallel experience, not a translation) and El Shoush (multi-language) both evidence this. It matters for the Gulf service areas (Jeddah, Riyadh) already listed on the Facebook page.
4. **There is a vertical asset nobody else in the city has** — a working maritime crewing platform, built in a Suez Canal port. See `[[Case Study - Osama Sakr Manning Agency]]`. This is the single strongest strategic asset the company owns.

**It does NOT prove these things — and no case study may claim them until it does:**

- **That anything got better for the client.** All four are described by *what they are*, never by *what changed*. There is not one number in the entire portfolio. This is Layer 5 of the diagnosis in `[[Foundation Brief]]` §2.
- **That clients are happy.** Zero client quotes exist. Two Facebook reviews exist with unknown content — see `[[Testimonial and Reference Playbook]]`.
- **Scale, uptime, or SLA performance.** The website claims `SLA Guarantees`; Facebook claims `99.9% uptime`. **Nothing in the evidence supports either.** Both are on the banned-phrase list in `[[Foundation Brief]]` §5 until a real SLA document and real monitoring data exist.
- **Healthcare or professional-services capability.** Beachhead segments C (healthcare) and D (professional offices) have **no proof at all**. Do not put a healthcare claim in a proposal.
- **Anything "worldwide" or "global".** The website footer says "growing enterprises worldwide". The evidence shows Egypt-based clients only. Banned until a non-Egypt/Gulf client exists.
- **Team size, years in business, or number of projects delivered.** Unknown. Never state a number.

**The gap in one line:** the company has four real systems and zero outcomes. Producing the outcomes is a phone-call problem, not a build problem.

## 3. Prioritised production order (proposed)

Ordered by *speed to publishable* × *revenue relevance*, not by project size.

| Order | Project | Why here | Blocked by | Realistic first output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Techno Square Platform** | Zero permission risk, live and demo-able, and it is the volume engine for beachhead B (education) in `[[Foundation Brief]]` §4. | **3** | **Osama Sakr Manning Agency** | Highest strategic value — it unlocks the maritime beachhead, the recommended lead market. Worth the permission wait. | **Written permission not on file.** Send the request today — see `[[Client Permission and Evidence Pack]]`. | Anonymised version ("a manning agency in Port Said") publishable immediately if permission is slow. |
| **4** | **El Shoush Travel System** | Real external proof, but travel is not a named beachhead. Useful as a second logo and as evidence of booking-system capability. | **Written permission not on file.** | Anonymised version, then named once permission lands. |

**Do 1 and 2 this week — they need nobody's approval but the founder's.** Send the permission messages for 3 and 4 on the same day, so both clocks run in parallel.

**Hard dependency:** publishing case studies onto a site with an expired TLS certificate and no server-side rendering wastes them — no visitor, search engine, or AI engine can read the page. Priority 0 in `[[Foundation Brief]]` §9 comes first. Until the site is fixed, publish case studies **as Facebook carousels and as PDF one-pagers attached to proposals**, where they can actually be seen.
