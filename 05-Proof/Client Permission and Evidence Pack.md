# Client Permission and Evidence Pack

## For future Claude

The practical kit for **legally and comfortably** publishing client work. Two external clients — El Shoush and Osama Sakr — have **no written permission on file**, which is why both case studies are frozen at `publishable: pending-permission`.

Two things this note is not: it is not legal advice, and it is not a threat mechanism. The tone throughout is *a partner asking a favour with a clear benefit attached*, not a vendor invoking a clause. In Port Said B2B, the relationship is worth more than the case study.

`❓ NEEDS FROM FOUNDER:` Do any signed contracts with El Shoush or Osama Sakr already contain a publicity, reference, or confidentiality clause? **Check the contracts before sending anything.** If a confidentiality clause exists, permission must be obtained in writing regardless of how friendly the relationship is; if a publicity clause already grants it, this whole process is a courtesy notification instead of a request.

---

## 1. The permission request message

Send by **WhatsApp** first — it is the channel both clients already use and it gets read. Email only as a formal follow-up carrying the consent form.

### Arabic (Egyptian dialect — send this one)

> أستاذ [الاسم]، إزيك يا فندم 🙏
>
> إحنا في Code Square بنجهّز دلوقتي صفحة أعمالنا ونشتغل على توثيق المشاريع اللي عملناها، وحابين — بإذنك — نتكلم عن النظام اللي بنيناه لحضرتك.
>
> اللي بنطلبه بالظبط:
> ١. نذكر اسم الشركة والشعار (اللوجو)
> ٢. نعرض صور من النظام (مع إخفاء أي بيانات خاصة بكم أو بعملائكم بالكامل)
> ٣. نذكر النتيجة برقم واحد بس — زي مثلاً الوقت اللي اتوفر أو عدد العمليات — والرقم ده حضرتك اللي بتديهولنا وبتوافق عليه
> ٤. جملة قصيرة من حضرتك عن تجربتك معانا — وطبعًا مش هتتنشر غير بعد ما حضرتك تقراها وتوافق على صيغتها بالحرف
>
> وأي حاجة من الأربعة دول حضرتك مش مرتاح ليها، نشيلها عادي جدًا ومفيش أي مشكلة. ولو حابين ما نذكرش الاسم خالص، ممكن نتكلم عن المشروع من غير أسماء.
>
> وفيها فايدة لحضرتك برضه: الصفحة بتوصل لشركات في نفس المجال، وبنحط لينك واسم الشركة فيها.
>
> لو موافق، هبعتلك ورقة موافقة قصيرة (صفحة واحدة) توقّعها وتبعتهالي صورة، وخلاص.
>
> شكرًا لوقتك يا فندم 🌹
> [الاسم] — Code Square

### English (for a client who prefers English or formal email)

> Dear [Name],
>
> I hope you are well.
>
> At Code Square we are currently documenting the projects we have delivered, and we would like — with your permission — to write about the system we built for you.
>
> Specifically, we are asking for:
> 1. Use of your company name and logo
> 2. Screenshots of the system, with all of your data and your customers' data fully removed or replaced with dummy data
> 3. **One** result figure — for example hours saved or operations processed — a number you provide and approve
> 4. A short sentence from you about working with us, published only in the exact wording you approve in writing
>
> If any of these four is not comfortable, we simply remove it — that is completely fine. If you would prefer we not name the company at all, we can describe the project without any identifying details.
>
> There is something in it for you as well: the page reaches companies in your sector, and we include your company name and a link.
>
> If you are happy to proceed, I will send a short one-page consent form to sign and send back as a photo.
>
> Thank you for your time,
> [Name] — Code Square

**Follow-up rule:** one polite follow-up after 5 working days, one after another 10. Then stop and publish the anonymised version. Do not chase a client three times for a marketing asset.

## 2. Written consent form (one page)

Keep it to one page. A long legal document from a small vendor reads as a trap and kills the yes.

```
نموذج موافقة على النشر — Code Square
CLIENT PUBLICATION CONSENT — Code Square

الشركة / Company: ______________________________
الشخص المفوّض / Authorised person: ______________________________
الصفة / Role: ______________________________
المشروع / Project: ______________________________
التاريخ / Date: ______________________________

أوافق على استخدام Code Square لما يلي في موادها التسويقية
(موقعها الإلكتروني، صفحات التواصل الاجتماعي، والعروض المقدمة للعملاء):

I consent to Code Square using the following in its marketing materials
(website, social media, and client proposals):

[ ] اسم الشركة                          Company name
[ ] شعار الشركة (اللوجو)                 Company logo
[ ] صور من النظام (بعد إخفاء البيانات)    System screenshots (data redacted)
[ ] الأرقام والنتائج المذكورة أدناه        The results/figures stated below
[ ] الاقتباس المذكور أدناه                The quote stated below
[ ] استقبال مكالمة مرجعية من عميل محتمل    Acting as a reference (taking a call)

الرقم / النتيجة المتفق عليها:
Agreed figure / result:
________________________________________________________

الاقتباس المتفق عليه (بالحرف):
Agreed quote (verbatim):
________________________________________________________
________________________________________________________

ملاحظات أو استثناءات / Notes or exclusions:
________________________________________________________

هذه الموافقة سارية حتى إشعار كتابي بالسحب. يمكن سحبها في أي وقت
بإرسال رسالة، وتلتزم Code Square بإزالة المحتوى خلال 7 أيام عمل.

This consent remains valid until withdrawn in writing. It may be withdrawn
at any time by message, and Code Square will remove the content within
7 working days.

التوقيع / Signature: ______________________________
```

**Rules of use:**
- A **ticked box** is the unit of permission. A client who ticks name and logo but not metrics has consented to name and logo only.
- A photo of the signed form sent by WhatsApp is acceptable. Do not demand a scan or a stamp.
- Store the signed form in the evidence folder (§5) and record the ticked items in the case-study note's Permission Status table.
- **If a client withdraws:** swap to the anonymised version within 7 working days, remove the logo everywhere including old social posts, and note the withdrawal date in the case-study frontmatter. Do not argue.

## 3. Anonymisation fallback

If a client declines or does not answer, publish the work anonymously. **The work is still proof; only the name is missing.**

| Client | Anonymised as | Still usable | Must be removed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Osama Sakr | **"a manning agency in Port Said"** — maritime crew recruitment | The full problem/approach/build narrative, the vertical positioning, the Port Said/Suez Canal context | Name, logo, any screenshot showing branding or real crew data, any figure the client did not approve |
| El Shoush | **"a travel agency"** (add city only if it does not identify them) | Booking and management capability, multi-language, Maps integration | Name, logo, branded screenshots, unapproved figures |

**The anonymisation test:** *could a person in that industry, in that city, name the client from this description alone?* If yes, it is not anonymised — it is a hint, and a hint without consent is worse than a name with consent. Drop a detail until the answer is no.

**What survives anonymisation, and it is a lot:** the sector, the class of problem, the architecture, the feature list, the methodology, and — if approved separately — the number. A prospect reading "a manning agency in Port Said" learns everything commercially relevant. **Do not treat the anonymised version as a consolation prize; for the maritime beachhead it is nearly as strong as the named one.**

## 4. Evidence-capture checklist — so this never happens again

The root cause of this whole problem is that evidence was never captured while the projects were live. Two fixes, both cheap.

### For CURRENT and FUTURE projects — build it into delivery

**At contract signature:**
- [ ] Include a publicity clause in every contract: *"العميل يوافق على استخدام اسم وشعار الشركة والإشارة إلى المشروع في المواد التسويقية لـ Code Square، على أن تُعتمد أي أرقام أو اقتباسات كتابيًا قبل النشر."* — permission by default, figures and quotes still approved individually. `❓ NEEDS FROM FOUNDER:` approve this clause and add it to the standard contract.
- [ ] Ask the reference-call question at signature, when goodwill is highest

**At kickoff (Discovery / التأسيس):**
- [ ] **Capture the "before" state** — photograph the spreadsheet, the paper form, the WhatsApp thread, the old system. *This is the single most valuable and most commonly lost artefact.* It cannot be recreated once the new system replaces it.
- [ ] Record the client's problem statement **in their own words**, verbatim, in the kickoff notes
- [ ] Record a baseline number: how long the task takes today, how many are processed per week, how many errors occur

**During the build:**
- [ ] Screenshot each major module as it ships, with dummy data
- [ ] Log architecture decisions and why they were made — this becomes "build in daylight" content (`[[Foundation Brief]]` §7)

**At launch:**
- [ ] Full screenshot set, dummy data, desktop and mobile
- [ ] Record the launch date

**30 days after launch:**
- [ ] Ask the single metric question and compare to the baseline
- [ ] Run the testimonial ask — see `[[Testimonial and Reference Playbook]]`
- [ ] Complete and file the consent form

**Rule:** a project is **not closed** until the evidence checklist is complete. Add it to the delivery definition of done.

### For the two PAST projects — retroactive capture

The "before" state is gone and cannot be recovered. Do this instead:
- [ ] Get permission (§1 and §2)
- [ ] Run the client interview (`[[Case Study Framework]]` §4) — their memory of the "before" is the only remaining source, and their words are more valuable than a photo would have been
- [ ] Capture screenshots now, with dummy data, in a screen-share with the client
- [ ] Ask for a **remembered** baseline and label it as one: "the client estimates it used to take about…"

## 5. Asset storage and naming

`❓ NEEDS FROM FOUNDER:` Where do these files live — Google Drive, a company machine, elsewhere? Pick one location and one owner. The convention below assumes a shared drive.

```
/Proof/
  /{client-slug}/
    /permissions/
      2026-09-02_{client-slug}_consent-signed.pdf
      2026-09-02_{client-slug}_quote-approval.png      ← the WhatsApp screenshot
    /screenshots/
      2026-09-02_{client-slug}_dashboard_desktop_01.png
      2026-09-02_{client-slug}_booking_mobile_01.png
    /before/
      2026-09-02_{client-slug}_before_spreadsheet.png
    /interview/
      2026-09-02_{client-slug}_interview_audio.m4a
      2026-09-02_{client-slug}_interview_notes.md
    /logo/
      {client-slug}_logo_dark.png
      {client-slug}_logo_light.png
```

**Conventions:**
- `{client-slug}` is lowercase, hyphenated, stable forever: `osama-sakr`, `el-shoush`, `techno-square`, `m-tech-square`
- Every filename starts with the **capture date** in `YYYY-MM-DD`, so files sort chronologically and an old screenshot is never mistaken for a current one
- **Never store a screenshot containing real client or end-customer data.** Redact or replace with dummy data at capture time, not later. For a manning agency this means real seafarer names, passport numbers, and certificates — treat those as sensitive personal data and never let them reach a marketing folder.
- One folder per client, including internal ones. Internal projects need the same evidence discipline even though they need no permission.
