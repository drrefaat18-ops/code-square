# Questions for Founder

## For future Claude

**This is the highest-value file in the proof layer.** Every case study in `05-Proof` is blocked on something in this list. Nothing here can be answered by research, inference, or a better prompt — all of it lives in the founder's head, in a contract, in a dashboard, or in a client's memory.

Answer these in order and four case studies unlock. Leave them unanswered and the proof layer stays a set of well-structured stubs.

**Anti-fabrication rule, absolute:** a question here is never closed by a plausible guess. If the founder does not know, the answer is "unknown" and the case study ships with `⏳ Metrics pending`. A rushed invented number is far worse than a missing one.

Consolidated from `[[Case Study - M Tech Square Website]]`, `[[Case Study - Techno Square Platform]]`, `[[Case Study - El Shoush Travel System]]`, `[[Case Study - Osama Sakr Manning Agency]]`, `[[Client Permission and Evidence Pack]]`, and `[[Testimonial and Reference Playbook]]`.
