# Messaging Framework — Code Square

## For future Claude

This note is the copy source of truth: the exact sentences Code Square says about itself, in Arabic and English, ordered from the single one-liner down to per-audience messages and elevator pitches. Read it before writing any post, ad, bio, email, proposal, or landing page — and read the **banned phrases** section before publishing anything. It executes [[Brand Positioning]]; tone and channel rules live in [[Brand Voice and Tone]].

> **Jargon defined once.** *Message hierarchy* = one top idea, supported by a few pillars, each backed by evidence — so every piece of copy ladders back to the same idea instead of saying something new each time. *Proof point* = the specific, checkable thing that makes a claim believable.

## 3. Elevator pitches

### 3.1 Ten seconds

- **AR:** بنبني النظام اللي شغلك بيمشي عليه — وبنفضل شغالين عليه معاك.
- **EN:** We build the system your business actually runs on — and we keep running it with you.

### 3.2 Thirty seconds

**AR (Egyptian dialect):**
> إحنا كود سكوير، من بورسعيد. بنشتغل زي فريق التكنولوجيا بتاع الشركة، بس من بره — يعني تكنولوجيا كاملة من غير ما توظف فريق.
> بنبدأ بفهم شغلك الأول، وبعدين بنبني النظام اللي محتاجه بالظبط، مش قالب جاهز.
> عملنا نظام لوكالة manning فيه توظيف وتتبع ولوحات متابعة لحظية، ونظام سفر وحجوزات، ومنصة تعليمية كاملة بتطبيق موبايل.
> وبعد الإطلاق مابنمشيش — بنفضل شغالين على النظام معاك.

**EN:**
> We're Code Square, based in Port Said. We work as your technology team from the outside — full engineering capability without hiring a team.
> We start by understanding how your business actually runs, then build the system it needs. No templates.
> We've built a manning-agency platform with recruitment, tracking and live dashboards; a travel booking and management system; and a full education platform with a mobile app.
> And we don't disappear at launch — we keep running the system with you.

### 3.3 Two minutes

**AR (MSA for formal/Gulf; switch to dialect for local conversation):**
> كود سكوير شركة برمجيات في بورسعيد، وجزء من مجموعة M Tech Square.
> **المشكلة التي نحلها:** معظم الشركات المتنامية تصل إلى نقطة يتوقف عندها الإكسل والبرامج الجاهزة عن الوفاء بالغرض. البيانات موزعة، لا أحد يملك صورة كاملة، والقرارات تُتخذ بالتخمين. الحل التقليدي هو توظيف فريق تقني كامل — وهو مكلف وبطيء ومخاطرته عالية.
> **دورنا:** نعمل كفريق المنتج والهندسة الخارجي لشركتك — Outsourced CTO. نبدأ بفهم عميق لطبيعة عملك، ثم نصمم المعمارية، ثم نبني، ثم نبقى معك بعد الإطلاق للتطوير والدعم. هذه منهجيتنا: التأسيس، المعمارية، الهندسة، الإطلاق والتطوير.
> **ما بنيناه فعلاً:** نظام إدارة لوكالة ملاحية يشمل التوظيف وتتبع المشاريع ولوحات تحليلات لحظية. نظام سفر وحجوزات متعدد اللغات مع تكامل الخرائط. منصة تعليمية متكاملة مع تطبيق جوال ولوحة تحكم ونظام تسجيل. وموقع المجموعة نفسه.
> **فلسفتنا، حرفياً:** لا نطارد الصيحات العابرة؛ بل نبني أسساً تدوم. لسنا مجرد مبرمجين؛ نحن مهندسو أنظمة. لسنا مجرد مزودي خدمة؛ نحن شريكك التكنولوجي.
> **الخطوة التالية:** جلسة تشخيص مجانية، ٣٠–٤٥ دقيقة. تخرج منها بورقة واحدة مكتوبة تحدد المشكلة والأولويات — سواء عملت معنا أو لا.

**EN:**
> Code Square is a software company in Port Said, Egypt, and part of the M Tech Square group.
> **The problem we solve:** growing businesses hit a point where spreadsheets and off-the-shelf tools stop working. Data is scattered, nobody has the full picture, and decisions get made on guesswork. The traditional fix is hiring a full technical team — expensive, slow, and risky.
> **What we do:** we work as your outsourced product and engineering team — an Outsourced CTO relationship. We start with a deep understanding of how your business runs, then design the architecture, then build, then stay with you after launch. That's our methodology: Discovery, Architecture, Engineering, Launch & Iterate.
> **What we've actually built:** an agency management platform for a maritime manning agency — recruitment, project tracking, live analytics dashboards. A multi-language travel booking and management system with Maps integration. A complete education platform with a mobile app, dashboard and enrolment flow. And the M Tech Square group site itself.
> **Our philosophy, in our own words:** We don't chase trends, we build fundamentals that last. We don't just code, we architect for scale. We're not just service providers, we're your technology partner.
> **Next step:** a free 30–45 minute diagnostic session. You leave with a written one-page summary of the problem and the priorities — whether or not you work with us.

⚠️ Two-minute pitch rules: never state a founding year, headcount, client count, or revenue figure — all TBD. Never name El Shoush or Osama Sakr in *written, published* material until publication permission is on file; naming them verbally in a meeting is a founder judgement call.

## 4. BANNED PHRASES

From [[Foundation Brief]] §5, extended. A phrase is banned when it is **unfalsifiable** (no test could prove it wrong) or **unproven** (a specific claim with no artifact behind it). Both make a buyer discount everything else in the sentence.

| Banned phrase | Why it is banned | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| **أقوى فريق** | Unfalsifiable superlative. Every agency says it; it carries zero information. | Name what the team actually built. |
| **أفضل شركة** | Same — a claim about ranking with no ranking behind it. | "الشركة اللي عملت نظام الـ manning agency ده." |
| **حلول متكاملة** | Filler. Means nothing specific; describes every software company alive. | Name the actual system: "نظام حجوزات بتكامل خرائط." |
| **أحدث التقنيات** | Directly contradicts the company's own philosophy — "لا نطارد الصيحات العابرة." | Name the technology and why it was chosen. |
| **ثقة عملائنا** | Asserts trust instead of showing it, while zero clients are named publicly. | Show a named client and a number. |
| **"Awwwards-tier"** (currently live on the website) | A specific award-body comparison with no recognition on record. Checkable, and it fails the check. | Show the interface. Let it argue for itself. |
| **"99.9% uptime"** (currently live on Facebook + the site) | A precise engineering SLA number with no monitoring, no SLA document, and no owner behind it. Worse than vague — it is a measurable promise that can be caught false. | Describe the actual support arrangement, or publish a real SLA. |
| **"SLA Guarantees"** (currently live on the website) | A guarantee is a contractual term. None exists. | "دعم بعد الإطلاق" with the real response commitment. |
| **"worldwide" / "globally"** (currently in the site footer) | No client outside Egypt/Gulf exists. Easily disproven, and it makes a Port Said company sound unserious. | "من بورسعيد للقاهرة والخليج." |
| **"trusted by X+ companies"** / any client count | Number does not exist. TBD. Fabricating it is the single most damaging thing this brand could do. | Say nothing about count until the number is real. |
| **$19 / $49 / $99** | Mockup art from a SaaS infographic. It is not Code Square's pricing and never was. | Nothing, until [[Pricing]] is decided. |
| **"شريكك التكنولوجي" used alone as a headline** | Not false — but it is the exact sentence every agency in Egypt uses, so it positions nothing. | Keep it inside the philosophy statement where it has context; lead with the one-liner. |
| Capability lists as a headline ("Mobile · Web · SaaS · AI · UI/UX · Integration · Support") | A menu is not a message. It tells the buyer what you sell, never why they should care. | The one-liner, then one capability relevant to that buyer. |
| Any founding year, headcount, or revenue figure | All TBD. | `TBD` — and add it to [[Open Questions and Decisions Needed]]. |

**Standing rule ([[Foundation Brief]] §0.5):** every claim names the artifact that backs it. No artifact → no claim.

---

## 5. Copy checklist before publishing

1. Does it ladder back to the one-liner or one of the three pillars?
2. Is there a named proof behind every claim?
3. Does it contain a banned phrase?
4. Is there one idea, not seven?
5. Does it end with a question or a named next step? ([[Brand Voice and Tone]])
6. Arabic and English both written natively — not machine-translated?
7. Are all numbers real, and sourced?
