# Discovery Call Script — جلسة التشخيص

## For future Claude

A **runnable script**, not a description of one. Every customer-facing line is written in real Egyptian dialect exactly as it should be spoken, with an English gloss underneath for the reader's benefit — **the English is never said to an Egyptian client**. For Gulf prospects (Jeddah, Riyadh — `stated` service areas), use the MSA variants marked `[فصحى]`.

This covers rung 1 of the offer ladder in `[[Foundation Brief]]` §6: the free 30–45 minute **جلسة تشخيص**. The output is a one-page written diagnosis the prospect keeps whether they buy or not.

Hard rules baked into this script:
- We **diagnose**, we do not pitch. If you are talking more than 30% of the time, the session has failed.
- **No price is quoted on this call.** None. See `[[Pricing and Packaging]]`.
- No banned phrases (`[[Foundation Brief]]` §5).
- No client names unless written permission is on file.
- The one-pager is delivered within 48 hours. Promise it, then keep it.

---

## Part 0 — Before the call (15 minutes, non-negotiable)

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| What does the company actually do? | You cannot diagnose a shipping agency if you do not know what a shipping agency does. |
| Who is on the call — owner, manager, or IT? | Changes every question you ask. |
| Do they have a website / page / app already? Open it. | You will refer to it. |
| Any recent news, expansion, new branch, hiring? | The best opening line comes from here. |
| Which of our four projects is closest to them? | Not to brag — to know which questions matter. |
| Write the ONE thing you most want to learn. | Keeps the call from wandering. |

**Setup:** phone on silent and out of sight. Notes on paper or a second screen, never typing loudly into the call. Recording only with explicit permission.

## Part 1 — The opening and the frame (3 minutes)

The frame is the most important 60 seconds of the call. It sets the expectation that this is a diagnosis, not a sales pitch, and it buys you permission to ask hard questions.

**Greeting**

> **أهلاً يا [الاسم]، إزيك؟ … تمام الحمد لله.**
> **شكراً إنك خدت من وقتك النهاردة.**

*(Hello [name], how are you? … Good, thank God. Thanks for giving me your time today.)*

**Time check — always ask, never assume**

> **قبل ما نبدأ، معايا حضرتك كام دقيقة دلوقتي؟ أنا حاجز 40 دقيقة، بس لو وقتك أقل نظبّطها.**

*(Before we start — how many minutes do you have? I've blocked 40, but if you have less we'll adjust.)*

**The frame — say this in full, every time**

> **خليني أقولك الجلسة دي شغلها إيه، عشان نبقى متفقين.**
> **أنا مش هنا أبيعلك حاجة النهاردة، وصدقني مش هحاول.**
> **الجلسة دي اسمها "جلسة تشخيص". يعني أنا هسألك أسئلة كتير عن شغلك، وإيه اللي بيتعبك فيه، وإيه اللي بتحاول توصله.**
> **وفي الآخر، خلال يومين، هبعتلك ورقة واحدة مكتوب فيها إيه المشكلة من وجهة نظري بالظبط، وإيه أول خطوة عملية.**
> **الورقة دي بتاعتك سواء اشتغلنا مع بعض أو لأ — تاخدها وتنفذها بنفسك أو مع أي حد تاني، عادي جداً.**
> **الحاجة الوحيدة اللي بطلبها منك: كلمني بصراحة. لو الوضع وحش، قولي وحش. أنا مش هحكم على حد، أنا محتاج أفهم بس.**
> **تمام كده؟**

*(Let me tell you what this session is, so we're aligned. I'm not here to sell you anything today, and I honestly won't try. This is called a Diagnostic Session. I'll ask you a lot of questions about your business, what's painful in it, and what you're trying to reach. At the end, within two days, I'll send you one page with what I think the problem actually is and what the first practical step is. That page is yours whether we work together or not — take it and implement it yourself or with anyone else, totally fine. The only thing I ask: talk to me straight. If things are bad, say bad. I'm not judging anyone, I just need to understand. Okay?)*

**Permission to ask directly**

> **وممكن أسألك أسئلة تفصيلية شوية عن الفلوس والوقت وطريقة اتخاذ القرار عندكم — مش فضول، ده عشان الورقة اللي هبعتها تطلع مفيدة فعلاً مش كلام عام.**

*(And I may ask fairly detailed questions about money, time, and how decisions get made — not nosiness, it's so the page I send you is actually useful instead of generic.)*

`[فصحى]` for Gulf prospects:
> **قبل أن نبدأ، اسمح لي أن أوضح طبيعة هذه الجلسة. الهدف منها هو التشخيص وليس البيع. سأطرح عليك أسئلة تفصيلية عن طبيعة العمل والتحديات الحالية، وسأرسل لك خلال 48 ساعة صفحة واحدة تلخّص المشكلة من وجهة نظرنا والخطوة العملية الأولى — وهي لك سواء تعاونّا أم لا.**

## Part 2 — The question sequence

Five blocks, in this order. **Do not reorder.** You cannot cost a problem you have not described, and you cannot recommend anything before you know the constraints.

Notation: **[Q]** = ask it. **[P]** = probe — only if the answer was thin. **[L]** = listen for this signal.

---

### Block A — الوضع الحالي · Current state (8–10 min)

Goal: understand how the business actually runs today, in physical detail. Do not accept abstractions.

**A1 — the opener**
> **حكيلي الأول، شغلكم بيمشي إزاي؟ يعني من أول ما العميل يتواصل معاكم لحد ما الشغلانة تخلص — إيه اللي بيحصل بالظبط، خطوة بخطوة؟**

*(Tell me first, how does your work actually flow? From the moment a client contacts you until the job is done — what exactly happens, step by step?)*

**[P]** > **وبعدين؟ … وده بيتسجّل فين؟ … ومين اللي بيعمل الخطوة دي؟**
*(And then? … And where does that get recorded? … And who does that step?)*

**A2 — the tools**
> **بتستخدموا إيه دلوقتي؟ إكسل، ورق، واتساب، برنامج جاهز، إيه؟**

*(What are you using today? Excel, paper, WhatsApp, an off-the-shelf program, what?)*

**[L]** — "إكسل" is the single most important word in this call. It means the workflow exists, is understood, and has outgrown its container. That is our best-fit customer.

**A3 — the volume**
> **في الشهر، بتشتغلوا على كام [شحنة / بحّار / حجز / عميل / طلب]؟**

*(Per month, how many [shipments / seafarers / bookings / clients / orders] do you handle?)*

**A4 — the people**
> **كام واحد بيلمس المعلومة دي قبل ما تخلص؟**

*(How many people touch this information before it's done?)*

**A5 — the manual patch**
> **في حاجة بتعملوها بإيديكم كل يوم وحاسين إنها المفروض متتعملش كده؟**

*(Is there something you do by hand every day that you feel shouldn't be done that way?)*

**A6 — the previous attempt** *(critical — never skip)*
> **جربتوا قبل كده تعملوا حاجة تحل ده؟ حصل إيه؟**

*(Have you tried before to build something to solve this? What happened?)*

**[L]** — if they were burned before, everything in `[[Objection Handling]]` about "we tried before and it failed" applies, and you must surface it now rather than let it ambush you at proposal time.

---

### Block B — المشكلة وتكلفتها · The problem and its cost (10–12 min)

Goal: get from "it's annoying" to a number. Numbers are what make a budget appear later. This is the block junior salespeople skip and it is the block that closes deals.

**B1 — the biggest pain**
> **من كل اللي حكيته لي، إيه أكتر حاجة بتوجع فعلاً؟ اللي لو اتحلت بكرة الصبح هتفرق معاك حقيقي؟**

*(Out of everything you told me, what actually hurts most? The thing that if it were solved tomorrow morning would genuinely change things for you?)*

**B2 — the time cost**
> **الحاجة دي بتاخد منكم قد إيه في الأسبوع؟ لو حسبناها بالساعات تقريباً؟**

*(How much time does this take you per week? Roughly, in hours?)*

**[P]** > **ومين اللي بيقضي الساعات دي؟ حضرتك ولا حد من الفريق؟**
*(And who spends those hours? You, or someone on the team?)*

**[L]** — if the owner is spending the hours, the cost is enormous and they usually know it.

**B3 — the error cost**
> **بيحصل غلط في الحتة دي؟ آخر مرة حصلت، كلّفت إيه؟**

*(Do mistakes happen at that step? Last time it happened, what did it cost?)*

**B4 — the lost-opportunity cost**
> **في شغل جالكم ومقدرتوش تاخدوه عشان الطريقة الحالية مش مستحملة أكتر من كده؟**

*(Has work come to you that you couldn't take because the current way can't handle more?)*

**[L]** — this is the strongest cost of all. Capacity you cannot sell is money already on the table.

**B5 — the growth blocker**
> **لو الشغل زاد الضعف بكرة، إيه أول حاجة هتقع؟**

*(If the work doubled tomorrow, what's the first thing that would break?)*

**B6 — the human cost**
> **الموضوع ده بيأثر على الفريق إزاي؟ حد بيزهق منه ولا بيتشد؟**

*(How does this affect the team? Is anyone frustrated or stressed by it?)*

**B7 — the client-facing cost**
> **العميل بتاعك بيحس بالمشكلة دي؟ ولا لسه جوه بس؟**

*(Does your client feel this problem? Or is it still internal?)*

**B8 — the do-nothing cost** *(the most important question in the entire call)*
> **لو مبقاش في حاجة اتعملت خالص، وفضل الوضع زي ما هو سنة كمان — إيه اللي هيحصل؟**

*(If nothing at all gets done, and things stay exactly as they are for another year — what happens?)*

**[L]** — if the honest answer is "لا حاجة، هنكمّل عادي" (nothing, we'll carry on fine), there is no project here. Be glad you found out in minute 25 instead of month three. Note it and move toward a graceful close.

---

### Block C — النتيجة المطلوبة · Desired outcome (5–7 min)

Goal: get them to describe success in their own words, so you can quote those words back in the proposal.

**C1 — the picture**
> **تخيل معايا إن المشكلة دي اتحلت. الصبح بيبقى شكله عامل إزاي؟ إيه اللي اتغير بالظبط؟**

*(Imagine with me that this is solved. What does the morning look like? What exactly changed?)*

**C2 — the measurable**
> **إزاي هتعرف إن الموضوع نجح؟ يعني إيه الرقم أو الحاجة اللي لما تشوفها تقول: آه، ده اشتغل؟**

*(How will you know it worked? What's the number or the thing you'd see and say: yes, this worked?)*

**[L]** — write this answer down verbatim. It becomes the success metric in the scope document and it is the number you go back for at go-live for `[[Portfolio and Case Studies]]`.

**C3 — the priority**
> **لو ينفع نحل حاجة واحدة بس في أول شهر، تكون إيه؟**

*(If we could only solve one thing in the first month, what would it be?)*

**C4 — the emotional driver**
> **وده ليه مهم دلوقتي بالذات؟ ليه مش السنة اللي فاتت ومش السنة الجاية؟**

*(And why is this important right now specifically? Why not last year and not next year?)*

**[L]** — the answer here is the real urgency. No urgency = the deal will stall no matter how good the fit.

---

### Block D — المحددات · Constraints (8–10 min)

Four constraints, always all four: money, time, decision, technical. Ask them plainly — the frame in Part 1 already bought you permission.

#### D1 — Budget (see the dedicated section below for the indirect route)

Direct version, when the rapport is good:
> **بصراحة كده، حضرتك حاطط ميزانية للموضوع ده؟ مش عايز رقم بالظبط، عايز أعرف بس إحنا في أي نطاق عشان أقترح حاجة واقعية مش أضيع وقتك.**

*(Straight up — have you set a budget for this? I don't want an exact number, I just want to know what range we're in so I propose something realistic instead of wasting your time.)*

If they refuse a number, do **not** push. Use the indirect questions below.

#### D2 — Timeline
> **عايز الحاجة دي تكون شغالة إمتى؟ في حاجة بتحكم التاريخ ده — موسم، عقد، شهادة، حاجة كده؟**

*(When do you want this working? Is there something driving that date — a season, a contract, a certification, something?)*

**[P]** > **ولو اتأخرت شهرين عن التاريخ ده، هيحصل إيه؟**
*(And if it slipped two months past that date, what happens?)*

**[L]** — a soft deadline means a soft deal. A contract renewal, an audit, or a season is a hard deadline and a real driver.

#### D3 — Decision process
> **لو قررنا نمشي في الموضوع ده، مين غيرك المفروض يكون موافق؟**

*(If we decided to go ahead, who besides you needs to agree?)*

> **وعندكم القرار بياخد وقت قد إيه عادةً في حاجة زي دي؟**

*(And how long does a decision like this usually take with you?)*

> **آخر مرة اشتريتوا حاجة شبه دي — إيه اللي حصل بالظبط؟ مين وقّع؟**

*(The last time you bought something similar — what exactly happened? Who signed?)*

**[L]** — the third question is the honest one. People describe an idealised process in questions 1 and 2 and the real one in question 3.

#### D4 — Technical
> **في أنظمة شغالة عندكم لازم الحاجة الجديدة تتكلم معاها؟ حسابات، ERP، حاجة عند الميناء، حاجة عند عميل؟**

*(Are there systems running that the new thing has to talk to? Accounting, ERP, something at the port, something at a client?)*

> **مين عندكم بيفهم في التكنولوجيا؟ في حد شغال IT ولا لأ؟**

*(Who on your side understands technology? Is there anyone doing IT or not?)*

> **الداتا بتاعتكم النهاردة موجودة فين؟ وحالتها إيه — نضيفة ولا مبعثرة؟**

*(Where does your data live today? And what condition is it in — clean or scattered?)*

> **في أي قيود على إن الداتا تكون على السحابة ولا لازم تفضل عندكم؟**

*(Any restrictions on data being in the cloud versus staying on-premise?)*

---

### Block E — الأولويات · Priorities (3–5 min)

**E1**
> **لو قسمنا الشغل ده على مراحل، وقلنا أول مرحلة تحل جزء واحد بس — يكون إيه؟**

*(If we split this into phases, and the first phase solves just one piece — which piece?)*

**E2**
> **إيه الحاجة اللي لو معملناهاش خالص، مفيش مشكلة؟**

*(What's the thing that if we never build it, is no problem at all?)*

**E3**
> **في حاجة مهمة عندك ومسألتش عنها؟**

*(Is there something important to you that I didn't ask about?)*

**[L]** — E3 catches the thing they came into the call wanting to say and never got to. Never skip it.

## Part 3 — Questions that reveal budget without asking for a number

Some Egyptian SMB owners will not state a budget, especially on a first call. It is not evasion — it is often that no budget has been formed at all. These questions get you a usable range without ever demanding a figure.

| # | Question (AR) | English | What the answer tells you |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **الشغل ده بيكلفكم كام في الشهر دلوقتي بالطريقة الحالية؟** | What does this cost you monthly the current way? | The ceiling. Nobody pays much more than the pain costs. |
| 2 | **قد إيه ساعة الشخص اللي بيعمل ده بتكلّف؟** | What does an hour of the person doing this cost? | Hours × rate = the hard savings number for the proposal. |
| 3 | **حد جالكم بعرض قبل كده على حاجة شبه دي؟ كان عامل إزاي؟** | Has anyone quoted you before for something like this? How was it? | Their reference price and their reaction to it. |
| 4 | **لو الحل ده وفّرلك [رقم من B2/B3] في الشهر، كنت تعتبره يستاهل قد إيه؟** | If this saved you [number] a month, what would you consider it worth? | Anchors on value, and they answer with a number. |
| 5 | **آخر استثمار عملتوه في الشغل — كان في إيه؟** | What was the last investment you made in the business? | Their real appetite and their approval ceiling. |
| 6 | **الحاجة دي هتتصرف من ميزانية إيه عندكم؟ تشغيل ولا مشاريع ولا حاجة تانية؟** | Which budget would this come from? Operations, projects, something else? | Whether a budget exists at all, and whose it is. |
| 7 | **لو الرقم طلع أكبر من المتوقع، ده قرارك لوحدك ولا محتاج حد معاك؟** | If the number came in higher than expected, is that your call alone or do you need someone? | Authority ceiling and the real decision-maker. |
| 8 | **هل بتفضّل تدفع مرة واحدة ولا على دفعات على مدار المشروع؟** | Do you prefer to pay once or in instalments across the project? | Cash-flow reality — often the true constraint, not the total. |

**The reframe when they will not answer at all:**
> **تمام، مش لازم رقم. خليني أقولها بطريقة تانية: في حاجات بتتعمل بمستوى بسيط، وفي حاجات بتتعمل بمستوى أوسع. أنا هحطلك في الورقة الاتنين، وانت تقولي أنهي واحد قريب من اللي في دماغك.**

*(Fine, no number needed. Let me put it differently: some things get built at a simple level and some at a broader level. I'll put both in the page for you, and you tell me which one is closer to what's in your head.)*

## Part 5 — Diagnosing what they actually need

Four possible answers, plus a fifth that is often the right one. Decide from the notes, not from what the prospect asked for. **Almost every SMB asks for a website. Most of them need an internal system.**

| Diagnosis | The signals in the notes | What we recommend |
|---|---|---|
| **موقع · Website** | Problem is being *found* and *believed*. Block B answers are about credibility, competitors' sites, clients asking "فين موقعكم؟". Internal workflow is fine. Volume low. No repeat-use need. | A focused site with one job. Small, fast, and honest. Do not oversell this into a platform. |
| **تطبيق · Mobile app** | Users come back repeatedly. Something needs a phone-only capability — camera, location, notifications, offline, on-site data entry. Users are in the field, not at a desk. | App — but almost always *after* the system that feeds it exists. An app on top of nothing is an expensive shell. |
| **MVP · منتج أولي** | They are building a *product to sell to others*, not to run their own operation. Idea stage. Needs to test whether anyone wants it. Multiple customers imagined. | Smallest thing that proves demand. Cite our own published position: 80% of users of a booking app only needed search + book + confirm (`stated`, Facebook post). One version, not the dream. |
| **نظام داخلي · Internal system** ← **most common, most valuable** | **Excel.** Data re-typed into multiple places. WhatsApp used as a database. Several people touching one process. Owner cannot see status without asking someone. Errors have a cost. Growth blocked by admin capacity. | The operating system of their business. This is the Outsourced CTO sale and it is where our proof lives — Osama Sakr Manning Agency is exactly this shape (`stated`). |
| **مش دلوقتي · Not now** | Block B8 answer is "nothing would happen". No date. No budget. No decision path. Or the problem is a people/process problem software will not fix. | Say so honestly. Send the one-pager anyway. Set a re-engagement date. This is a win, not a loss. |

**The overlap rule:** when two apply, name both and phase them. "المرحلة الأولى نظام داخلي، والمرحلة التانية تطبيق للفريق اللي برة" is a better answer than choosing one and being wrong.

## Part 6 — Presenting the recommendation without pitching

Do this on the call verbally, then repeat it in the one-pager. Four moves, in order.

**Move 1 — Play it back in their own words.** Nothing convinces like being accurately heard.

> **خليني أرجّعلك اللي فهمته، وصحّحلي لو غلطت في حاجة.**
> **انتوا بتشتغلوا على [الحجم] في الشهر، والعملية دلوقتي ماشية على [الأدوات]، وبتعدّي على [العدد] أشخاص.**
> **أكتر حاجة بتوجعك هي [الوجع بكلامه بالحرف]، وده بياخد منكم حوالي [الساعات] ساعة في الأسبوع.**
> **ولو الوضع فضل زي ما هو سنة كمان، اللي هيحصل هو [إجابته على سؤال B8].**
> **صح كده ولا فاتني حاجة؟**

*(Let me play back what I understood, and correct me if I got anything wrong. You handle [volume] a month, the process today runs on [tools], and it passes through [number] people. The thing that hurts most is [their exact words], and it takes about [hours] hours a week. And if things stayed as they are for another year, what happens is [their B8 answer]. Is that right, or did I miss something?)*

**Move 2 — Name the real problem, and be willing to disagree with them.**

> **بصراحة، من اللي سمعته، المشكلة عندكم مش [اللي هو طلبه]. المشكلة إن [التشخيص الحقيقي].**
> **يعني لو عملنا [اللي طلبه] بس، الوجع اللي حكيتلي عليه هيفضل زي ما هو.**

*(Honestly, from what I heard, your problem isn't [what they asked for]. The problem is [the real diagnosis]. Meaning if we only did [what they asked for], the pain you described would stay exactly as it is.)*

This line is uncomfortable and it is the most valuable line in the call. It is the difference between a vendor and a technology partner — and "شريكك التكنولوجي الاستراتيجي" is already our published philosophy (`stated`, `[[Foundation Brief]]` §1).

**Move 3 — Give the direction, not the design.**

> **الاتجاه اللي أنا شايفه: نبدأ بـ[المرحلة الأولى] عشان [السبب المربوط بوجعه]، وبعدها [المرحلة التانية].**
> **مش هدخل معاك في تفاصيل الشاشات والمميزات دلوقتي — عشان الكلام ده لو اتقال بسرعة كده بيطلع غلط، وأنا مش عايز أديك أرقام في الهوا.**

*(The direction I see: start with [phase one] because [reason tied to their pain], then [phase two]. I won't go into screens and features with you now — because said quickly it comes out wrong, and I don't want to give you numbers out of thin air.)*

**Move 4 — Prove it with the one thing that is actually ours.** Only if it genuinely fits, and only within permission.

For maritime/logistics:
> **إحنا شغالين على نظام لوكالة توظيف بحري هنا في بورسعيد — توظيف وتتبع ولوحات متابعة لحظية. يعني الشكل ده من الشغل إحنا عارفينه من جوه مش من برة.**

*(We work on a system for a maritime manning agency here in Port Said — recruitment, tracking, and live monitoring dashboards. So this shape of work we know from the inside, not from the outside.)*

For education:
> **عندنا منصة تعليمية شغالة فعلاً — بتطبيق موبايل ولوحة تحكم وتسجيل. أقدر أوريهالك شغالة دلوقتي مش سلايدات.**

*(We have an education platform actually running — with a mobile app, a dashboard, and enrolment. I can show it to you live, not slides.)*

> **⚠️ Permission gate:** El Shoush and Osama Sakr may only be **named** once written permission is on file. Until then describe the work without the client's name: *"نظام لوكالة توظيف بحري في بورسعيد"*. The two internal projects (M Tech Square, Techno Square) need no permission. See `[[Portfolio and Case Studies]]`.

**What never happens in Move 4:** a features list, a technology list, or a claim with no artifact behind it. Nothing from the banned list in `[[Foundation Brief]]` §5 — no أقوى فريق, no أفضل شركة, no أحدث التقنيات, no 99.9% uptime.

## Part 7 — The transition to the paid Blueprint

Only after Move 3 has landed and they have not objected to the diagnosis.

**The bridge**
> **بص، الخطوة الطبيعية بعد الكلام ده مش إني أبعتلك عرض سعر. لأن أي رقم أقوله لك دلوقتي هيكون تخمين، وأنا مش بشتغل كده.**

*(Look, the natural step after this conversation isn't for me to send you a quote. Because any number I say now would be a guess, and I don't work that way.)*

**Name and frame the Blueprint**
> **اللي بنعمله عندنا اسمه "خريطة المشروع". أسبوع لأسبوعين، بنقعد مع الناس اللي شغالة على العملية فعلاً، ونطلع بحاجة مكتوبة:**
> **— المشكلة متعرّفة بالظبط**
> **— رحلة المستخدم من أولها لآخرها**
> **— المميزات مرتبة بالأولوية، إيه اللي في المرحلة الأولى وإيه اللي يستنى**
> **— والنطاق مقسّم على مراحل، وكل مرحلة عليها سعر ثابت**
> **يعني بتخرج منها بمستند تقدر تنفذه معانا أو مع أي حد تاني — وبتعرف بالظبط بتدفع في إيه.**

*(What we do is called the Project Blueprint. One to two weeks, we sit with the people who actually run the process, and we come out with something written: the problem defined exactly; the user journey start to finish; features ordered by priority — what's in phase one and what waits; and the scope split into phases, each with a fixed price. You come out with a document you can execute with us or with anyone else — and you know exactly what you're paying for.)*

**The de-risk — the reason this rung exists at all**
> **وحاجة مهمة: فلوس الخريطة بتتخصم من قيمة البناء لو مشينا مع بعض. يعني انت مش بتدفع مرتين.**

*(And something important: the Blueprint fee is credited against the build cost if we go ahead together. So you're not paying twice.)*

**The honesty that earns the sale**
> **وبصراحة، فيه ناس بتخلص الخريطة وتقرر ماتبنيش دلوقتي. ودي نتيجة كويسة برضه — أحسن ما تدفع فلوس بناء في حاجة مش متعرفة كويس.**

*(And honestly, some people finish the Blueprint and decide not to build now. That's a good outcome too — better than paying to build something that was never properly defined.)*

**Price on the call:** do not state one. If asked directly, see `[[Pricing and Packaging]]` — the current answer is `TBD` and until it is set, the line is:

> **الرقم هبعتهولك مكتوب في العرض عشان يبقى واضح ومحدد، مش رقم بقوله بسرعة في مكالمة. بس أقدر أطمّنك إنه رقم مصمم عشان يبقى أقل بكتير من إنك تبدأ بناء وانت مش متأكد.**

*(I'll send you the number written in the proposal so it's clear and specific, not a number I say quickly on a call. But I can assure you it's designed to be far less than starting a build while you're unsure.)*

## Part 8 — Close and next step

Never end without a date. "هبعتلك وأشوف رأيك" is not a next step.

**Confirm the commitment**
> **تمام. خلينا نتفق على الخطوتين الجايين:**
> **١. أنا هبعتلك ورقة التشخيص خلال يومين — يوم [التاريخ] بالظبط. فيها المشكلة زي ما فهمتها والاتجاه اللي أنا شايفه.**
> **٢. ومعاها عرض خريطة المشروع بالنطاق والسعر.**
> **وبعدين نتكلم مكالمة قصيرة، ٢٠ دقيقة، أراجعهم معاك وتقولي رأيك بصراحة — حتى لو رأيك لأ.**
> **يومك إيه أحسن، [يوم] ولا [يوم]؟**

*(Right. Let's agree on the next two steps: 1. I'll send you the diagnosis page within two days — [date] exactly. It has the problem as I understood it and the direction I see. 2. And with it, the Blueprint proposal with scope and price. Then we talk for a short call, 20 minutes, I walk you through them and you tell me honestly what you think — even if what you think is no. Which day suits you better, [day] or [day]?)*

**Lock the channel**
> **أبعتلك على الواتس ولا على الإيميل؟**

*(Should I send it to you on WhatsApp or email?)*

**Ask for the sceptic** — the single most underused closing question:
> **آخر حاجة: مين عندكم ممكن يبقى معترض على الفكرة دي؟ بسأل عشان أجاوب على اعتراضه في الورقة نفسها بدل ما ييجي بعدين ويوقف الموضوع.**

*(One last thing: who on your side might object to this idea? I'm asking so I can answer their objection in the page itself instead of it coming later and stalling things.)*

**Thanks — no flattery**
> **شكراً على وقتك وعلى صراحتك، دي فرقت معايا فعلاً في إني أفهم الصورة.**

*(Thank you for your time and your honesty, that genuinely helped me see the picture.)*

**If it is not a fit — say it on the call.** This is a rule, not an option.
> **بصراحة معاك، من اللي سمعته أنا مش شايف إن اللي بنعمله هو الحل الصح ليكم دلوقتي. اللي انت محتاجه أقرب لـ[البديل]. هبعتلك الورقة برضه لأني وعدتك بيها، وفيها اللي أنا شايفه. ولو الوضع اتغير بعدين، أنا موجود.**

*(Being straight with you — from what I heard I don't think what we do is the right answer for you right now. What you need is closer to [alternative]. I'll still send you the page because I promised it, and it has what I see. And if things change later, I'm here.)*

You lose nothing. You gain a person who tells others you were honest — which in Port Said is a distribution channel.

## Part 9 — Red flags: disqualify

Any two of these together = disqualify. Any one marked 🔴 alone = disqualify.

| # | Flag | What it actually means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🔴 B8 answer is "nothing would happen" | No pain. No project. |
| 2 | 🔴 "عايز حد يعملهولي بالنسبة من الأرباح" (equity/revenue share instead of payment) | They want a co-founder, not a vendor. See `[[Objection Handling]]`. |
| 3 | 🔴 Refuses any budget signal after all eight indirect questions | Either no money or no trust. Either way, not now. |
| 4 | 🔴 Wants a fixed final price today, before any scoping, and will not accept the Blueprint | Guaranteed scope dispute later. |
| 5 | 🔴 Has burned two or more previous vendors and every story is entirely the vendor's fault | Pattern. You will be the third story. |
| 6 | Cannot name who signs, and cannot get you to them | No path to a decision. |
| 7 | No date, no driver, "لما ربنا ييسر" | Real intent, no urgency. Nurture, don't pipeline. |
| 8 | Wants an exact copy of a competitor's system, exactly, cheaper | Price shopper. |
| 9 | Asks for free wireframes/mockups "عشان نشوف شغلكم" before any paid step | Free-work extraction. Offer the portfolio instead. |
| 10 | Their real problem is a person, not a process | Software will not fix it and will get blamed. |
| 11 | Budget clearly and permanently below our minimum (`TBD`) | Refer out kindly. |
| 12 | Demands we start before anything is written down | The scope dispute has already begun. |

**How to disqualify without burning the relationship:** use the "not a fit" script above, send the one-pager anyway, and set a 6-month re-engagement reminder per `[[Sales Playbook]]` Part 7. People remember who was honest with them for free.
