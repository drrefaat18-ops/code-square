## 1. The Inviolable Truth Hierarchy

Every AI agent must strictly respect the source hierarchy:

```
00-Source/ (Immutable historical evidence & documents)
      ↓
00-Source/Foundation Brief.md (CANONICAL STRATEGY — wins any conflict)
      ↓
01-Identity/ through 10-Group/ (Derived operational notes)
      ↓
Conversations/ & Logs/ (Working debates, daily execution, audit logs)
```

1. **`00-Source/` is immutable:** Files like `2026-09-02 - Facebook Page Evidence.md` and `2026-09-02 - Website Evidence...md` record exact historical snapshots. **Never rewrite or edit them.**
2. **`[[Foundation Brief]]` is canonical:** If any note in `01-Identity` through `10-Group` contradicts `Foundation Brief.md`, the Brief wins until the founder explicitly updates it.
3. **Derived notes can be refined:** Any note in folders 01–10 may be sharpened, updated, or expanded, provided it adheres to the Brief and factual evidence.

### 2.2 Pricing Rules
- **Mockup Art Alert:** The pricing tiers `$19 / $49 / $99` found on old infographics are purely UI mockups. **Never quote them as real prices.**
- Real custom software pricing for Code Square is bespoke/TBD based on scope, milestones, and discovery calls.

### 2.3 Banned Buzzwords (Forbidden Arabic & English Claims)
Never use hollow, unprovable claims in any copy, proposal, or strategy:
- ❌ **أقوى فريق** (The strongest team)
- ❌ **أفضل شركة** (The best company)
- ❌ **حلول متكاملة** (Integrated end-to-end solutions)
- ❌ **أحدث التقنيات** (Cutting-edge technologies)
- ❌ **ثقة عملائنا** (Our clients' unyielding trust — unless quoting an attested review)
- ❌ **"Awwwards-tier"**
- ❌ **"99.9% uptime"** or **"SLA Guarantees"** (until a binding SLA legal document is drafted and signed)
- ❌ **"Worldwide / Globally"** (Code Square serves Egypt and the Gulf; no global client exists)

### 2.4 Confidence Labels
When asserting facts in notes or proposals, tag statements with:
- `stated` — directly extracted from `00-Source/` or stated by the founder.
- `verified` — independently checked and proven in a live session.
- `researched` — backed by an external URL and date.
- `proposed` — an agent recommendation (must never be framed as existing fact).
- `TBD` — unknown fact needing founder resolution.

## 6. Rules of Engagement for AI Systems

1. **Read Before Writing:** Always read existing notes in full before editing or referencing them. Avoid guessing file contents.
2. **Keep the Vault Clean:**
   - Do NOT litter the root directory with scratch files or arbitrary notes.
   - Place strategic specs in `docs/superpowers/specs/` and plans in `docs/superpowers/plans/`.
3. **Propagate Changes:**
   - If you write a new note, add it to `00-START-HERE.md` and log the entry in `Logs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
   - If you resolve a `TBD`, remove it from `[[Open Questions and Decisions Needed]]`.
4. **Communicate for the Founder:**
   - Structure responses with executive summaries, visual comparison tables, and unambiguous action items.
   - Speak human-to-human. Respect the founder's time and healthcare background.
