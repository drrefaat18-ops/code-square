# Buyer Personas

## For future Claude

Six archetypes derived from `[[ICP - Ideal Customer Profiles]]`. **Every name below is fictional and every persona is a composite archetype — none of these are real people, and none of them are Code Square clients.** Never cite a persona as evidence, a testimonial, or a reference.

Personas are **proposed** (2026-09-02). They are hypotheses to be corrected by real discovery calls. After every real sales conversation, come back and fix the persona — especially the objections and the opening lines, which are the parts most likely to be wrong.

Pairing rule used here: each segment gets **an economic buyer** (says yes to the money) **and a blocker** (cannot say yes but can say no). Selling to only one of the two is the most common way a deal in this market dies.

---

## How to read a persona

| Field | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Their job title and what they are actually measured on |
| **A day in the life** | Where the software would sit in their real routine |
| **What keeps them up at night** | The fear you sell against — not the feature you sell |
| **How they buy software** | The actual purchase path, which is rarely the one a website assumes |
| **Objections** | What they will say out loud, plus the honest answer |
| **What they read / watch** | Where a message can reach them |
| **Opening line** | The first sentence of an outreach message. Arabic first, because these buyers are Egyptian. |

**A note on the opening lines.** They are deliberately short, specific, name a workflow rather than a service, and ask a question. Consistent with `[[Foundation Brief]]` §5 banned phrases: no أقوى فريق, no حلول متكاملة, no أحدث التقنيات. If an opening line could be sent by any other agency in Egypt, it is a bad opening line.

## Persona 1 — "Captain Hossam" · Managing Director, shipping / manning agency
**Segment A · ECONOMIC BUYER · archetype, not a real person**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / background** | 52. Ex-seafarer or the son of the founder. Knows ships, not software. |
| **Company** | Port Said shipping or manning agency, 18 staff, family-owned, second generation partly involved. |
| **Measured on** | Client retention with shipowners, and not losing money on a port call. |

**A day in the life.** Arrives at 8. Three WhatsApp groups are already loud — one per vessel in port. Signs documents he has not read because someone needs them now. Takes a call from a principal in Piraeus or Dubai asking for a status update he has to go and ask two people to assemble. Leaves at 7 with the same open items he had at 8.

**What keeps him up at night.** That a mistake nobody notices until it is expensive — an expired certificate, a missed filing, an under-estimated disbursement — costs him a principal he spent nine years earning. And that the whole operation runs on two employees' memories.

**How he buys software.** He does not shop. He asks another agency owner who they use, or his son shows him something. He needs to meet the vendor **in person**, in his own office, and see a face he can hold accountable. He signs on trust and a handshake, then wants a contract that matches what was said. He will not read a proposal longer than three pages.

**Objections, and the honest answer.**

| He says | He means | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| "دي هتكلف كتير" | I cannot see what I get for the money | Show one workflow and its cost today in hours per week. Sell the Blueprint (`[[Pricing and Packaging]]`), not the build. |
| "الشغل عندنا مختلف" | Templates have failed here before | Agree with him. That is literally the site's own line — "لا قوالب جاهزة هنا" (**stated**). |
| "الناس مش هتستخدمه" | My staff resisted the last system | True and fair. Scope V1 to one team, one workflow, four weeks. |
| "خلينا نأجل لبعد الموسم" | Not urgent enough yet | Attach it to a dated trigger — the e-invoicing deadline, or the near-miss he already told you about. |
| "ممكن حد يعمللي ده بـ ٢٠ ألف" | A freelancer quoted me | Do not argue on price. Ask who maintains it in month seven. See `[[Competitive Landscape]]`. |

**What he reads / watches.** Almost no software content. Maritime trade press, Chamber of Shipping circulars, WhatsApp groups of other agency owners, LinkedIn occasionally. **He is not reachable by content marketing. He is reachable by referral and by a visit.**

**Opening line (AR):**
> أ. حسام، أنا [الاسم] من Code Square في بورسعيد. إحنا بنينا نظام لوكالة أسامة صقر للتوظيف البحري — تتبع الشهادات والانتهاءات ولوحة متابعة لحظية. سؤال واحد: عندكم دلوقتي مين اللي بيتابع تواريخ انتهاء الشهادات، وعلى إيه؟

**Opening line (EN, for a principal-facing or Gulf contact):**
> We built the crewing and certificate-tracking system for a manning agency here in Port Said. One question before I take any more of your time: today, who tracks certificate expiries at your office, and in what?

## Persona 2 — "Amira" · Operations Manager, shipping / manning agency
**Segment A · BLOCKER · archetype, not a real person**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / background** | 34. Nine years at the firm. Knows every file, every principal, every exception. |
| **Measured on** | Nothing going wrong. Invisible when she succeeds. |

**A day in the life.** She *is* the system. The master Excel sheet is hers. The colour codes are hers. When the MD needs a number, he asks her. She has not taken a full week of leave in three years because nobody else can run the sheet.

**What keeps her up at night.** Two opposite fears at once: that the sheet fails and it is her fault, and that a new system makes her replaceable. **Both must be addressed or she will quietly kill the project.**

**How she buys software.** She does not buy it. She **approves or sabotages** it. She will be in the second meeting, will ask the operational questions nobody else thought of, and her verdict reaches the MD the same evening.

**Objections, and the honest answer.**

| She says | She means | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| "النظام ده مش هيعرف يتعامل مع الحالات الاستثنائية" | You will simplify away the real work | Ask her for the three worst exceptions and design for them in front of her. This is the fastest way to convert her. |
| "مين هيدخل البيانات القديمة؟" | You are about to hand me a second job | Answer it concretely: migration scope, who does it, how long. Never wave it away. |
| "الشغل هيقف أسبوع" | Fear of a live cutover during a port call | Parallel-run plan, in writing. |
| Silence in the meeting | She has decided against it | Get her alone. Ask what she would build. |

**Convert her by making her the owner of the system, not its subject.** Named in the project as the workflow owner. The dashboard she gets is *her* dashboard. This costs nothing and decides the deal.

**What she reads / watches.** Facebook and Instagram, practical YouTube, Excel tips. She is the persona most reachable by useful content — a real reason for Code Square to publish workflow-level material rather than "ما هو الـ UX".

**Opening line (AR):**
> أ. أميرة، سؤال سريع مش بيع: لو غبتي أسبوع، الشيت اللي بتشتغلي عليه حد تاني يعرف يفتحه ويكمل؟ إحنا بنبني الأنظمة اللي بتشيل الحتة دي — وعايز أفهم الحالات الاستثنائية عندكم قبل ما أتكلم عن أي حاجة تانية.

## Persona 3 — "Ustaz Tarek" · Owner, private training academy
**Segment B · ECONOMIC BUYER · archetype, not a real person**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / background** | 41. Was the star teacher; now runs the business and still teaches the top groups. |
| **Company** | Academy with ~700 students across two branches, 14 part-time instructors. |
| **Measured on** | Enrolment count each term, and cash. |

**A day in the life.** Teaches in the afternoon and evening. Between sessions, answers dozens of WhatsApp messages from parents about schedules and fees. Sunday is the schedule-rebuild day, done in Excel, always late. Term-start week is chaos he has normalised.

**What keeps him up at night.** That students are attending without paying and he cannot prove it. That a competitor academy with a slicker app pulls parents away next term. That he cannot open a third branch because he personally is the coordination layer.

**How he buys software.** Fast, emotionally, and comparatively. He wants to **see it working** — not a slide. He will decide inside two meetings if he can click through a real product. **This is exactly what Techno Square lets Code Square do** (**stated**).

**Objections, and the honest answer.**

| He says | Answer |
|---|---|
| "الأهالي مش هيستخدموا التطبيق" | Do not start with the parent app. Start with payments and attendance — the parts that pay for themselves. |
| "أنا عندي جروبات واتساب وشغالة" | Agree, and cost the leakage: ask how many students attended last term without full payment. Usually nobody knows, which *is* the answer. |
| "ادفعلك من الحجز الجديد؟" | No. Deferred payment against future enrolment is a disqualifier (`[[ICP - Ideal Customer Profiles]]` B.6). |
| "بكام في الشهر بس؟" | Route to the pricing decision in `[[Pricing and Packaging]]` — do not improvise a monthly number. |

**What he reads / watches.** Facebook groups for academy owners, TikTok and Reels for marketing ideas, WhatsApp broadcast from suppliers. **Highly reachable by content and by a demo video.**

**Opening line (AR):**
> أ. طارق، الترم اللي فات كام طالب حضر من غير ما يكمل دفع؟ لو الإجابة "مش عارف بالظبط" — دي بالظبط الحتة اللي بنحلها. عندنا منصة شغالة فعلاً (تكنو سكوير) أقدر أوريهالك في ١٥ دقيقة، من غير عرض سعر ومن غير التزام.

## Persona 4 — "Mona" · Academy administrator / reception
**Segment B · BLOCKER · archetype, not a real person**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / background** | 27. Runs registration, collects fees, answers the phone, knows every parent's name. |
| **Measured on** | Nothing formally. Judged on complaints. |

**A day in the life.** Registration book, cash box, WhatsApp, and a queue of parents — simultaneously. Peak load at term start is genuinely unmanageable and everyone accepts it as normal.

**What keeps her up at night.** Being blamed for a cash discrepancy she cannot reconstruct. And a system that makes every mistake she makes permanently visible.

**How she affects the buy.** Her opinion after the demo is the owner's tiebreaker. If she says "ده هيسهل عليا", it closes. If she says "ده هيبقى شغل زيادة", it stalls.

**Objections, and the honest answer.** "هيبقى شغل زيادة" → demo the *registration* screen, not the admin dashboard, and time it against her current process on a stopwatch. That single comparison converts her.

**What she reads / watches.** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook. Not reachable by B2B content — reachable only inside the demo.

**Opening line (AR):**
> أ. منى، ممكن تفرجيني إزاي بتسجلي طالب جديد دلوقتي، خطوة بخطوة؟ عايز أقيس الوقت اللي بياخده — عشان لو النظام مش هيوفّر عليكي وقت فعلاً، مش هنبنيه.

## Persona 5 — "Dr. Nourhan" · Owner-pharmacist, small pharmacy chain
**Segment C · ECONOMIC BUYER · archetype, not a real person**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / background** | 38. Pharmacist. Owns four branches built over eleven years. Still works a shift. |
| **Measured on** | Margin, and shrinkage she cannot explain. |

**A day in the life.** Works a shift at the main branch. Fields WhatsApp from three branch managers about stock, staff, and prices. Reviews distributor invoices in the evening. Does a monthly manual stock-take at one branch at a time, so the chain-wide picture never exists at a single moment.

**What keeps her up at night.** The expiry write-off she takes every quarter and cannot forecast. That branch managers hold information she does not. That growing to six branches multiplies a problem she has not solved at four.

**How she buys software.** Skeptically, and from someone who speaks her language. She has been sold to by non-pharmacists many times. **The founder being a pharmacist is not a nice detail here — it is the entire reason the meeting happens** (**stated** — `[[Foundation Brief]]` §3).

**Objections, and the honest answer.**

| She says | Answer |
|---|---|
| "عندي برنامج صيدلية بالفعل" | Correct — and it works per branch. Ask what it tells her about *all four* branches at once. Position as the consolidation layer, not a rip-and-replace. |
| "البيانات دي حساسة" | Legitimate. Written data-handling agreement, named hosting, defined access. Do not hand-wave. Egypt's Law 151/2018 is relevant (**TBD** — confirm executive regulations before contracting). |
| "المدراء مش هيدخلوا البيانات" | True unless entry is a by-product of work they already do. Design for that or do not build it. |
| "ممكن أجرب فرع واحد؟" | **Say yes.** This is the pilot Segment C needs (`[[Foundation Brief]]` §7). Trade a discount for a documented before/after and a filmed testimonial. |

**What she reads / watches.** Pharmacist Facebook groups, syndicate communications, distributor reps. **A distributor rep introduction beats any advertisement.**

**Opening line (AR):**
> د. نورهان، أنا صيدلي كمان — وده السبب إني بسأل السؤال ده بدل أي كلام تاني: آخر جرد، الفروع كانت شايفة مخزون بعضها ولا كل فرع لوحده؟ لو كل فرع لوحده، الدوا اللي بيتلف عندك في فرع غالبًا ناقص في فرع تاني.

## Persona 6 — "Ahmed" · Branch manager / head pharmacist
**Segment C · BLOCKER · archetype, not a real person**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / background** | 44. Ten years running the busiest branch. Trusted, autonomous, proud. |
| **Measured on** | His branch's numbers, informally. |

**A day in the life.** Runs his branch as a small kingdom. Orders on relationships with reps. Handles his own shortages by phoning the branch he likes best. His judgment *is* the process.

**What keeps him up at night.** That a central system exposes his branch's numbers to comparison, and that head office starts making decisions he used to make.

**How he affects the buy.** He does not sign, but he supplies the data. A system he resents receives late, wrong, or partial entries — and then dies of "the data isn't accurate", which is what everyone will blame on Code Square.

**Objections, and the honest answer.** "ده هيبقى رقابة" is the real objection, said out loud as "مش هيبقى دقيق". Answer it directly: give him a transfer request tool that gets him stock from another branch in minutes. Make the first thing the system does for him a thing that helps *him*. Control follows, and he tolerates it.

**Opening line (AR):**
> أ. أحمد، لما يخلص عندك صنف، بتعمل إيه دلوقتي؟ بتكلم مين وبياخد قد إيه؟ لو قدرنا نخلي ده يتم في دقيقتين من الموبايل، ده يفرق معاك؟

## The economic buyer / blocker pairing map

| Segment | Economic buyer | Blocker | The one thing that decides the deal |
|---|---|---|---|
| A · Maritime | Captain Hossam (MD) | Amira (Ops Manager) | Making Amira the named owner of the system rather than its subject |
| B · Education | Ustaz Tarek (Owner) | Mona (Admin) | A live demo of the registration screen, timed against the current process |
| C · Healthcare | Dr. Nourhan (Owner-pharmacist) | Ahmed (Branch manager) | Giving the branch manager a benefit before giving head office visibility |
| D · Professional | Managing partner (**not profiled** — expansion segment, see `[[ICP - Ideal Customer Profiles]]` §D) | The other partners | Consensus. Which is why it is not the beachhead. |

## Rules that apply to every persona

1. **Every opening line names a workflow, not a service.** "بنعمل مواقع وتطبيقات" gets no reply. "مين اللي بيتابع تواريخ انتهاء الشهادات؟" gets a reply.
2. **Ask a question, do not pitch.** Consistent with the voice rule in `[[Foundation Brief]]` §8: every message ends with a question or a named next step.
3. **Arabic first, Egyptian dialect, for all Egyptian buyers.** English or MSA for Gulf and for principal-facing correspondence. Never machine-translate.
4. **Never open with price.** Route every price question to the diagnostic session, then the Blueprint (`[[Pricing and Packaging]]`).
5. **Never claim a client Code Square has not published with permission.** Osama Sakr and El Shoush may not yet be nameable — check `[[Open Questions and Decisions Needed]]` before using either name in outreach.
6. **After every real call, update this note.** These are hypotheses. A persona that has survived ten real conversations unchanged has almost certainly not been tested honestly.
